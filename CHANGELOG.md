## 1.2.0 · release candidate (not yet published)

- A new look on every screen: warm cream market mat, pastel fruit lanes, coin prices and round buttons
- A short market opening plays when you open the game (about 2.5 seconds, tap to skip)
- A new pixel-art farmer's lorry with a wooden roof and a string of flags, and market stalls with tiled roofs in each player's colour
- The table is bigger and clearer: rivals sit across from you, one column per fruit, freshness shown by colour, and the farmer's lorry unloads every morning
- Pick one of six shopkeepers before the game; each has one perk and no two players share one
- New favour cards: Gossip (see tomorrow's walk-ins) and Scout (peek at one of today's). Good Riddance is gone
- Market Days and a Closing Day give each game a rhythm, and prices are posted like a real market stall
- How to play starts with the big idea and follows one customer's order from start to finish
- Easier to read: Trophies, tips, chips and buttons have clearer text and a slightly darker terracotta
- Online: undo your buys on your turn, and change your name in the lobby
- Rules version 6: update the app to play online (older versions cannot join), and a game in progress from 1.1.1 can't be resumed

## 1.1.1 · published 2026-10-27 (`83de99b`, tag v1.1.1)

- Fixed: after reloading the page or rejoining an online match, the game could stay stuck on "Connecting to room…", especially on slower phones or when the app was in the background

- Behind the scenes: updates now reach players only after a preview and an owner's approval, with automatic checks before and after

## 1.1.0 · published 2026-10-26 (`c915e49`)

_Published before this workflow by the old automatic deploy; summarised from git history._
