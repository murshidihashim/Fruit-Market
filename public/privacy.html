Fruit Market: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Fruit Market is a game made by murshidihashim. Accounts are optional. It has no ads and no third-party trackers.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved game, statistics, achievements, stamp book, looks and daily challenge scores are kept in the app's local storage. The statistics are plain counts: games finished and won, wins with each shopkeeper, pieces sold of each fruit, standing orders delivered in full and your personal bests in one game. Your stall level is worked out from these counts. The app also notes your last 12 finished games on the device, so that no game is counted twice; these notes are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this. If you have an account, a copy of your progress is also kept on our server.

Accounts (optional)

You can play without an account. If you make one, you choose an ID and a password. We do not ask for an email address.

We store your ID, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your progress: the counts described above, achievements, unlocked looks, the looks you chose (stall colours, sign frame, title, price tag, results stamp and sounds) and league stage. This lets your progress follow you to another device. It is kept on our game server (Cloudflare). We cannot read your password and we cannot recover it if you lose it, so please keep it safe.

Please do not use your real name or email address as your ID. Offensive IDs are blocked.

To sign out, use Settings. Signing out saves your progress to your account and clears it from that device. To delete your account, use Settings and choose Delete account, then enter your password. This removes your ID, password and saved progress from our server.

Your ID is not shown to other players. Other players online see your player name and the look you are wearing: your colours and items, sign frame, title, price tag and results stamp. The sounds you chose are heard only by you and are not sent.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If "Share anonymous stats" is on in Settings (it is on by default), the app sends our server short event counts such as "a game started", "day 5 was reached", "a game was won" or "this device is active 3 days after installing" (the install date itself stays on your device), plus short error messages if the app crashes. These are added to daily totals. They contain no name, account, device ID, advertising ID or location, and we do not store IP addresses with them. Daily totals are deleted after 90 days. Turn the setting off to stop sending them.

Online play

When you play online, the name you type, the look you are wearing and your moves are sent to our game server (Cloudflare) so the other players can see them. A random seat code is stored on your device so you can reconnect. Rooms are deleted 24 hours after the last move.

Daily challenge leaderboard

When you finish a daily challenge while online, the name you chose, your score and your moves for that game are sent to our server so it can check the score and show it on that day's leaderboard. To stop others using your name on the board, your device also sends a random key made on your device; the server stores only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, which cannot identify you. Leaderboards show names and scores only. Offensive names are replaced automatically.

Permissions

The app uses the internet for online play, the leaderboard and (optionally) anonymous statistics, and may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Players should not use their real full name or email address as their player name or ID.
