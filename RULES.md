# Fruit Market: Rules

*Game design by Murshidi bin Md Hashim. Digital edition rules (v1.0).*

Fruit Market is a game about buying and selling fruit. Each player runs a small fruit shop that buys from the farmers and sells to local customers. Fruit goes bad over time, so you have to turn it into cash fast. You can also undercut your rivals as low as you like.

## Goal

Have the **most cash** at the end of the last day. Fruit left on your shelf and upgrades you built are worth nothing at the end. Ties go to whoever sold more fruit.

## Players and length

- 2 to 4 shops. Each one is played by you, a friend on the same phone, or a computer player (Easy, Normal or Hard).
- **Quick** is 8 days, **Standard** is 10 days and **Long** is 14 days.
- Each shop starts with **$10**, an empty shelf of 8 spaces, and no favour cards.

## The fruit

| Fruit | Ripe days | Cost: Fresh / Okay / Not so | Unripe days | Price drop: Okay / Not so |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🫐 Blueberry | 3 (1 fresh, 1 okay, 1 not so) | $2 / $1 / $1 | none | −$1 / −$3 |
| 🍉 Watermelon | 5 (2 fresh, 2 okay, 1 not so) | $3 / $2 / $1 | 1 | −$2 / −$3 |
| 🍎 Apple | 7 (3 fresh, 3 okay, 1 not so) | $4 / $2 / $1 | 1 | −$2 / −$4 |
| 🥭 Mango | 7 (2 fresh, 3 okay, 2 not so) | $3 / $2 / $1 | 2 | −$2 / −$3 |

Every fruit sits on a **freshness track** at the farmers market and in each shop. Freshest is on the right, oldest on the left. **Every night, all fruit moves one space left.** Fruit that falls off the left end spoils and is thrown away.

**Unripe fruit** (optional rule) costs $1 at any stage. It sits to the right of fresh, can't be sold yet, and becomes fresh as the nights pass.

## Cards

- **Farmer cards (20):** each lists three fruits, 4 to 6 of each. The fruit not on the card still arrives, 2 of it. Some cards mark one fruit as arriving unripe. With fewer players the farmers bring less: 80% for 3 players, 60% for 2.
- **Customer cards (24):** a name and up to three fruits they want, each with an amount and the most they'll pay for a *fresh* one. With fewer players customers buy a little less.
- **Event cards (12):** one is drawn each morning from Day 2 onward.
- **Favour cards (12):** a secret reward for serving a customer's whole order by yourself. You can hold up to 3.

## A day

### 1. Morning
1. Draw an **event card** (not on Day 1) and apply it.
2. Draw a **farmer card** and add that fruit to the farmers market at the freshest position (or the unripe position).
3. Everyone can see the **expected customer** who arrives today. A second, **walk-in** customer is a surprise.

### 2. Buying
In turn order, each shop buys **up to 3 fruits** (5 with a Handcart) from the farmers market, paying the cost shown for that fruit's freshness. Your shelf holds 8 fruits (14 with a Bigger Shelf). Fruit keeps its freshness when it moves to your shelf. Unbought fruit stays at the market and keeps ageing.

The first buyer moves one seat clockwise each day.

### 3. Customers arrive
The expected customer and a walk-in join the market as **New today**. Customers who weren't fully served yesterday are still there as **Waiting (last day)**. The next expected customer is revealed as **Tomorrow**.

### 4. Selling (secret prices)
Every shop secretly decides, for each customer and each fruit they want, **how many to offer and at what price per fruit**. Then all offers are revealed at once.

- **The price cap:** you can't ask more than the customer pays for your fruit's freshness. If John pays up to $10 for fresh apples, an *okay* apple can sell for at most $8 and a *not so* apple for at most $6.
- There is **no lower limit** (except on a Minimum Price day). Sell for $1 if you want.
- When you offer several fruits, your **oldest fruit that is fresh enough for your price** goes first.
- The **cheapest offers sell first**, until the customer has what they want.
- **Ties:** the fresher fruit wins. If still tied, a player with Favoured Trade wins, then turn order.
- You're paid as soon as you sell.
- Waiting customers are served before new ones.
- **Favours:** if you serve a customer's entire remaining order by yourself, draw a favour card.

A new customer who doesn't get everything comes back tomorrow as *Waiting*. A waiting customer leaves after today either way.

### 5. Evening
In turn order, each shop may build **upgrades** and play a **Priority** favour.

### 6. Night
All fruit at the market and in every shop ages one step. Spoiled fruit is thrown away. Then the next day begins.

After the last day's selling, the game ends. Count your cash.

## Event cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| ⚖️ Minimum Price (×2) | Nobody may sell a fruit below the price shown on the card today. |
| 🌾 Good Harvest | Farmers bring 2 extra of every fruit. |
| 🐛 Bad Harvest | Farmers bring 2 fewer of every fruit. |
| 🎉 Festive Season | Every customer pays up to $2 more today. |
| 😴 Slow Season | Every customer pays up to $2 less today. |
| 📈 Price Hike | Every fruit at the farmers market costs $1 more today. |
| 🔥 Heatwave | All fruit ages one extra step tonight. |
| 🌧️ Rainy Day | No walk-in customer today. |
| 📱 Mango Craze / Blueberry Craze | Customers pay $3 more for that fruit today. |
| 🚚 Lorry Breakdown | No farmer delivery today. |

## Favour cards (2 of each)

| Card | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 💵 Free Cash | Your buying turn | Gain $3. |
| 🎁 Free Fruit | Your buying turn | Take any one fruit from the farmers market for free (doesn't count toward your 3). |
| ♻️ Good Riddance | Your buying turn | Sell all your ripe fruit back to the farmers market for $1 each. |
| 🧊 Fridge | Your buying turn | All your ripe fruit moves one step fresher. |
| 🤝 Favoured Trade | When you set prices | You win every tie today. |
| ⏩ Priority | Evening | You buy first tomorrow. |

## Upgrades (optional rule)

Each can be built once, in the evening.

| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 🗄️ Bigger Shelf | $5 | Hold 14 fruits instead of 8. |
| 🛒 Handcart | $6 | Buy up to 5 fruits a day instead of 3. |
| 🪧 Signboard | $8 | Earn a $1 tip on every fruit you sell. |
| ❄️ Cool Room | $9 | Your fruit ages only every other night. |

Cash spent on upgrades is gone from your score, so build early or not at all.

## Daily challenge

Everyone in the world plays the same shuffled decks each day: you against Rosa (Normal), Benny (Hard) and Kak Yah (Normal) over 10 days. Your best score for the day and your streak are saved on your phone.

## What changed from the paper draft

| Paper draft | Digital edition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Players take turns undercutting in the open | Everyone sets prices secretly, then they're revealed together | Faster on a phone, and computer players can't "see" your price |
| Paid when a customer card leaves the market | Paid immediately on each sale | Simpler to follow |
| 2 rounds of the first-player token | Fixed 8, 10 or 14 days | 2 rounds is only 4 to 8 turns, too short to plan |
| Farmer card fruits only | The missing fruit still arrives (2) | Prevents days where nobody can sell anything |
| Favour cards | Same six cards; Fridge now affects ripe fruit only | Keeps unripe fruit meaningful |
| Event cards (6 types) | 10 types (adds Heatwave, Rainy Day, Craze, Lorry Breakdown) | More variety and replay value |
| Unripe fruit (expansion idea) | Built in as an optional rule | From the design notes |
| (none) | Shop upgrades | A second way to invest your cash |
