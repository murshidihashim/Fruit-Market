# Fruit Market: Rules

*Game design by Murshidi bin Md Hashim. Tabletop edition rules (rules version 4).*

Fruit Market is a game about buying and selling fruit. Each player runs a small fruit stall that buys from the farmers and sells to the town. Fruit goes bad over time, so you have to turn it into cash fast. Every day ends with one question: **how much can I charge before my rival, or the rot, gets me?**

Every rule here can be played with cards and coins on a table; the app only does the bookkeeping.

## Goal

Have the **most cash** at the end of the last day. Fruit left on your shelf and specialities you built are worth nothing at the end. Ties go to whoever sold more fruit.

## Players and length

- 2 to 4 shops. Each one is played by you, a friend on the same phone, or a computer player (Easy, Normal or Hard).
- **Quick** is 8 days, **Standard** is 10 days and **Long** is 14 days.
- Each shop starts with **$10**, a shelf of 8 spaces, and no favour cards.

## The fruit

Every fruit uses the same freshness rule: it sells at the customer's full price when **fresh**, **$1 less when okay** and **$2 less when not so**. Fruits differ in how many nights they stay in each stage, and in price.

| Fruit | Play style | Track (oldest → freshest) | Cost: Fresh / Okay / Not so | Unripe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🫐 Blueberry | Cheap and popular, mushy in 3 days. Fast turnover. | not so · okay · fresh | $2 / $1 / $1 | none |
| 🍉 Watermelon | The steady seller. | not so · okay · okay · fresh · fresh | $3 / $2 / $1 | 1 night ($1) |
| 🍎 Apple | The keeper: lasts a week. Never the best price, never a loss. | not so · okay ×3 · fresh ×3 | $3 / $2 / $1 | none |
| 🥭 Mango | Buy green for $3, ripens in 2 nights, sells at a premium, spoils fast once ripe. | not so · okay · fresh · fresh | $4 / $2 / $1 | 2 nights ($3) |

Every night all fruit moves one space left. Fruit that falls off the left end spoils. Unripe fruit can't be sold yet and becomes fresh as nights pass.

## Cards

- **Farmer cards (20):** each lists three fruits, 3 to 5 of each, printed for 3 shops. The fruit not on the card arrives as 1. With **2 shops**, take 1 less of each listed fruit (minimum 1); with **4 shops**, add 1 of every fruit. Some cards mark one fruit as arriving unripe.
- **Customer cards (24):** a name and up to three fruits, each with an amount and the most they pay for a *fresh* one. The back of each card shows the fruits they want.
- **Event cards (13):** one is drawn each morning from Day 2 onward and shown a day early.
- **Favour cards (12):** a secret reward for serving a customer's whole order by yourself. Hold up to 3 (4 with the Charmer trait).

## The queue

Each morning shows tomorrow on the **Tomorrow strip**: the next farmer card, the next event, one **expected customer** face up, and the **walk-ins** face down. Walk-ins show only the fruit on their card backs.

- Walk-ins per day: **players − 2** in the first half of the game, **players − 1** in the second half.
- **Market Day:** one more walk-in (see below).

## A day in five beats

### 1. Dawn
1. Apply today's **event**.
2. The farmer's lorry unloads today's card at the freshest position (or the unripe position). No lorry on a Market Day.
3. Draw tomorrow's farmer card, event and queue for the Tomorrow strip.

### 2. Market
Each shop buys **up to 4 fruits** (5 with a Barrow), paying the cost for that fruit's freshness. Your shelf holds 8 (12 with a Barrow). **The poorest shop buys first** (ties go round the table; Day 1 is random). A Priority favour jumps the queue.

### 3. Open stalls
Today's customers arrive: yesterday's unserved customers (now **waiting, last day**), the expected customer and the walk-ins, who turn face up now. Each shop secretly chooses how many of each ripe fruit to put in its **for-sale crate**; then all crates are shown at once. Fruit you keep back stays on your shelf.

### 4. The bell
Each shop secretly sets **one price per fruit** it put out, then everyone reveals together.

- **Price cap:** a customer pays at most their card price for a fresh fruit, $1 less for okay and $2 less for not so. When you sell, your **oldest fruit that is fresh enough for your price** goes first.
- There is **no lower limit** (except on a Minimum Price day).

### 5. The rush
Customers buy in queue order: waiting customers first, then new ones. For each fruit a customer wants, they buy from the **cheapest stall** that has fruit fresh enough for its price; leftover demand **spills to the next stall**.

- **Ties:** Favoured Trade, then the Haggler, then fresher fruit, then the poorer shop, then turn order.
- You're paid as soon as you sell.
- **Favours:** serve a customer's entire remaining order by yourself and draw a favour card (at most one per day).
- A new customer who doesn't get everything comes back tomorrow as *waiting*. A waiting customer leaves after today either way.

### Evening and night
- **Evening:** play evening favours (Priority, Market Gossip). On the evening after each Market Day (days 3, 6, 9 and 12), draft **one stall speciality** from the four.
- **Night:** all fruit at the market and in every shop ages one step. Spoiled fruit is thrown away.

## Market rhythm

- **Market Days (days 3, 6, 9 and 12, never the last day):** one more walk-in, and no lorry. Stock you kept is all you have.
- **Last Call (final day):** customers pay $1 more. Afterwards the **jam maker** buys every shop's leftover ripe fruit for $1 each.

Market Days and Last Call are on by default and can be switched off in the set-up.

## Event cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| 🎉 Festival | Customers pay $3 more per fruit, and an extra walk-in comes. |
| 🫐 Blueberry Glut | Farmers bring 1 extra blueberry per shop, and blueberry customers pay $1 less. |
| 🔥 Heatwave | Watermelons sell for $4 more, but all fruit ages an extra step tonight. |
| 🍎 Apple Blight | No apples arrive today, and apple customers pay $1 more. |
| 📱 Mango Craze | Mangoes sell for $3 more, and an influencer wants 3. |
| 💪 Superfood Trend | Blueberries sell for $3 more, and the gym club wants 4. |
| 🌾 Good Harvest | Farmers bring 1 extra of every fruit for every 2 shops, and everything costs $1 less. |
| 🐛 Bad Harvest | Farmers bring half as much (rounded up). Fruit sells for $2 more. |
| 📈 Price Hike | Every fruit at the farmers market costs $1 more. |
| ⚖️ Minimum Price | Nobody may sell below: watermelon $5, apple $5, mango $6, blueberry $4. |
| 🕵️ Health Inspector | Tonight every shop pays $2 for each not-so fruit on its shelf. Never the last day's event. |
| 🚚 Lorry Breakdown | No farmer delivery today. |
| 🏨 Hotel Order | A hotel chef wants 3 apples, 2 watermelons and 2 blueberries. |

## Favour cards (2 of each)

| Card | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 👂 Market Gossip | Evening | Secretly look at the front of every face-down walk-in in tomorrow's queue. Don't show anyone; everyone sees that you played it. |
| 🎁 Free Fruit | Your buying turn | Take any one fruit from the farmers market for free. |
| ♻️ Good Riddance | Your buying turn | Sell all your ripe fruit back to the farmers market for $1 each. |
| 🧊 Fridge | Your buying turn | All your ripe fruit moves one step fresher. |
| 🤝 Favoured Trade | At the bell | You win every tied price today. |
| ⏩ Priority | Evening | You buy first tomorrow. |

## Stall specialities

Drafted on the evening after each Market Day. Each can be built once, and each changes what you do.

| Speciality | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| ❄️ Cold Room | $4 | Your fruit ages only every other night, and heatwaves can't touch it. |
| 🧃 Juice Bar | $3 | +$1 for each okay or not-so fruit you sell. Each night up to 3 fruits that would spoil become juice: $2 each. |
| 🛒 Barrow | $6 | Buy up to 5 fruits a day and hold 12. |
| 🪧 Premium Display | $8 | Your fresh fruit may be priced $1 above a customer's normal limit. |

## Merchant traits

On the first morning, each shop is dealt 3 traits and keeps one for the whole game. Everyone can see everyone's trait.

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Haggler | You win tied prices, even against fresher fruit. |
| Big Basket | Buy 1 extra fruit every day. |
| Bargain Hunter | Your first fruit each day costs $1 less. |
| Charmer | Start with 3 favour cards, and hold up to 4. |
| Local Favourite | Customers you serve completely by yourself tip you $4. |
| Nest Egg | Start with $15 instead of $10. |

## Playtest options

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| Core market | No traits, favour cards or specialities: just buying, stalls and the bell. |
| Street prices (beginner) | Every customer pays one street price per fruit (fresh): blueberry $5, watermelon $7, apple $6, mango $9. Cards with the "generous" icon pay $2 more. |
| Market pulse (advanced) | After each day, a fruit that ran short gets a +$1 price marker (up to +$2); a fruit with a glut at the market gets −$1 (down to −$1); otherwise the marker drifts back to 0. |

## Daily challenge

Everyone in the world plays the same shuffled decks each day: you against Foxy Rosa (Normal), Big Benny (Hard) and Patches (Normal) over 10 days. Scores are kept per rules version.

## What changed from the paper draft

| Paper draft | This edition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Players take turns undercutting in the open | Crates shown first, then one secret price per fruit revealed at the bell | One clear dramatic moment a day |
| Paid when a customer card leaves the market | Paid immediately on each sale | Simpler to follow |
| 2 rounds of the first-player token | Fixed 8, 10 or 14 days | Long enough to plan |
| Farmer card fruits only | The missing fruit still arrives (1) | Prevents days where nobody can sell anything |
| Event cards (6 types) | 13 events, each announced a day early | Events change decisions, not just numbers |
| First player rotates | Poorest shop buys first | Stops early leaders running away |
| (none) | Traits, specialities, Market Days, Last Call | Every game opens differently and has an arc |
