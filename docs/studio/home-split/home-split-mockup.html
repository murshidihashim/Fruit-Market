What changed in this round

  • Top to bottom: fruit banner, title and profile, the four mode tabs, Rosa and her bubble, one wide card, Play, the bottom bar.
  • Nothing said twice. The mode's name is on its tab and its card only. Rosa no longer repeats rules; she chats. Play just says Play.
  • Wide card. The first customer and the lorry sit side by side, the fruit counts are about twice the size, and the rules are one line at the foot.
  • "Not played" is now plain words beside the date, so only Play looks like a button.
  • Tabs are one joined strip above Rosa and the card. Swiping the card still works.
  • Polish: fruit sits straight on its colour in the banner, with a thin white edge (a blueberry on blue would vanish without it); the gear has its own square; small labels are darker.
  • Kept: Rosa full body and moving, the swipe, one Play button, the same button routes.

Where each button leads

The same rule on every card: the card tells you about the mode, Play opens that mode's own page, and the choices are made there.

Seen on the live game

  • The four ways to play are on Home's card and again on the Play page, with different lines under them.
  • The Daily and Sprint cards have large empty gaps; the League card is full. The cards do not feel like one set.
  • Modes change only by tapping the four tabs. A swipe on the card does nothing.
  • The shopkeepers are small circles (36 to 44 px). Their full stall pictures show only on set-up pages.
  • Nothing on Home moves after the opening.

Limits to know

  • Her body below the shoulders is new art, drawn for this mockup. Her face, hair, forearm and price tag are the game's own drawing.
  • Her chat lines are new words and my suggestion. Two of them use the customers' own descriptions from the game (Coral Mei is not fussy; Grandpa Tan remembers ten-cent apples).
  • The Custom card lists the four ways as words, not buttons. Play opens the Play page. Quick Match goes from one tap to two.
  • The next card now peeks in by only a sliver, so the tabs are the main way to change mode.
  • The "Reduced motion" setting would hold her still.

Three things to decide

  1. Is this layout the one to build? If yes, I write the plan for your approval.
  2. Are Rosa's look and chat lines right? Tap through all four modes. Change any wording you like.
  3. May the four buttons leave the Custom card? Play then always opens the Play page. Quick Match becomes two taps.
