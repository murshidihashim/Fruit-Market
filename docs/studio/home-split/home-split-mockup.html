What changes

  • Left: your own stall on top (it replaces the small profile chip), and under it the shopkeepers you would face in the card showing. They bob, walk in when you swipe, and say their real lines from the game.
  • Right: one card per mode. Swipe, or tap a name under the cards. The next card peeks in so the swipe is easy to find.
  • One Play button for the card showing, where your thumb is. It becomes Continue when that mode has a game waiting.
  • Custom match no longer has four buttons on the card. Play opens the Play page with the four ways, as it does today.
  • The lanes, the title, Settings and the bottom bar stay where they are.

Where each button leads

Seen on the live game

  • The four ways to play are on Home's card and again on the Play page, with different lines under them.
  • The Daily and Sprint cards have large empty gaps; the League card is full. The cards do not feel like one set.
  • Modes change only by tapping the four tabs. A swipe on the card does nothing.
  • The shopkeepers are small circles (36 to 44 px). Their full stall pictures show only on set-up pages.
  • Nothing on Home moves after the opening.

Limits to know

  • The phone is upright, so the left side is narrow (about 150 of 390). Cards get shorter text than today; the detail stays on each mode's page.
  • The shopkeeper pictures are single drawings. They can bob, nod, walk in and talk in bubbles now. Waving arms or blinking needs new drawings.
  • Quick Match goes from one tap to two.
  • The "Reduced motion" setting would stop the movement, as in the rest of the game.

Three things to decide

  1. Is this split right on an upright phone? Left about 40%, right about 60%, as shown. The other choice is characters across the top and cards under them, which gives wider cards.
  2. May the four buttons leave the Custom card? Play then always opens the Play page. Quick Match becomes two taps.
  3. Is moving the existing pictures enough for a first version? Or do you want new drawings (arms, blinking) planned as their own piece of work?
