What changes

  • Left: Rosa, full body, with no frame round her. She walks in, bounces, blinks, tilts her head, waves her price tag and taps her foot.
  • Her bubble explains the mode on the card showing. When you swipe, she gives a small hop and explains the new one. Tap her for a second line.
  • Right: one card per mode. Swipe, or tap a name under the cards. The next card peeks in.
  • One Play button for the card showing. It becomes Continue when that mode has a game waiting.
  • Custom match no longer has four buttons on the card. Play opens the Play page with the four ways, as today.
  • The lanes, the title, your profile chip, Settings and the bottom bar stay.

Where each button leads

The same rule on every card: the card tells you about the mode, Play opens that mode's own page, and the choices are made there.

Custom matchPlay → the Play page → Quick Match (pick shopkeeper and table size, then the search) · Private Room (your room, or join with a code) · Computer (set-up) · Pass & Play (set-up).

Seen on the live game

  • The four ways to play are on Home's card and again on the Play page, with different lines under them.
  • The Daily and Sprint cards have large empty gaps; the League card is full. The cards do not feel like one set.
  • Modes change only by tapping the four tabs. A swipe on the card does nothing.
  • The shopkeepers are small circles (36 to 44 px). Their full stall pictures show only on set-up pages.
  • Nothing on Home moves after the opening.

Limits to know

  • Her body below the shoulders is new art. The game's picture of Rosa stops at the chest. I drew the apron, skirt, legs and shoes for this mockup; treat them as a sketch to approve or redraw.
  • Her face, hair, arm and price tag are the game's own drawing, moved as they are.
  • Her words are new and are my suggestion. They need your approval like any other text.
  • On a 360 x 640 phone the cards are 166 px wide, so they carry less text than today. The detail stays on each mode's page.
  • Quick Match goes from one tap to two.
  • The "Reduced motion" setting would hold her still.

Three things to decide

  1. Is her full-body look right? Yellow apron, red skirt, white top as on her card. Say what to change, or approve it as the base for the real drawing.
  2. Are her lines about each mode right? Swipe through all four and tap her on each. Change any wording you like.
  3. May the four buttons leave the Custom card? Play then always opens the Play page. Quick Match becomes two taps.
