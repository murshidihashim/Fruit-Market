Fruit Market · card mock-up concepts

Two frame concepts for the Look E printed cards, shown at real proportions (63 × 88 mm cards, cut corners). Pick one frame, or mix parts. These are design mock-ups, not print files.

Design only (DEC-063)Grey shapes = portrait placeholdersBack fruit order = proposal
For you to decide
  1. Frame: Concept 1 (crate label) or Concept 2 (market poster), or a mix of parts.
  2. Customer backs: show the wanted fruit always in the order blueberry, watermelon, apple, mango, each in its own fixed spot? (Today each card uses its own order.)
  3. Title font: Alfa Slab One (Concept 1) or Lilita One (Concept 2). Fredoka stays for numbers, prices and text.

Concept 1 · Crate label

Old fruit-crate label: banner, seal, sunburst, notched corners. Warm and decorative.

Customer deck

Shopkeeper deck

What makes it a crate label

  • Gold banner with the name, a numbered seal, notched corners.
  • A soft sunburst behind every portrait.
  • Display face: Alfa Slab One (names, titles). Fredoka for numbers, prices and text.
  • Customer frame: terracotta on every customer card. Shopkeeper frame: teal.
  • Sizes: portrait 25 mm tall (customers), 43 mm (shopkeeper); order circles 5 mm; prices 14 pt; smallest text 6 pt.

Concept 2 · Market poster

Market poster: one bold colour band, then a clean, quiet frame. Calmer and easier to read.

Customer deck

Shopkeeper deck

What makes it a market poster

  • A bold colour band across the top, then a plain, clean frame.
  • Portrait window is the flat mat colour, no sunburst.
  • Display face: Lilita One (names, titles). Fredoka for numbers, prices and text.
  • Ink frame on every card. Teal band for customers, terracotta for shopkeepers.
  • Sizes: portrait 25 mm tall (customers), 43 mm (shopkeeper); order circles 5 mm; prices 17 pt; smallest text 6 pt.
Every customer card in a concept has the same frame, edge and bleed colour, and the freshness type is a word in the same place on every card (hidden-information rules, art direction section 9). Prices, wants and totals come from src/engine/data.ts after today's price shift. Colours: Look E palette only (src/ui/palette.ts); the smallest text is pure black so it prints sharply. Fruit are the game's own pixel sprites, drawn as sharp squares.
