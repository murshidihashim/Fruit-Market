### DEC-025 · Market board: vertical decay tracker and a delivery lorry

- **Question:** How should the market look and how should fruit arrive each day?
- **Choice:** The market is a vertical decay tracker: one column per fruit, left to right (blueberry, watermelon, apple, mango); new and ripening fruit at the top, spoiling fruit at the bottom; each slot shows its price, and empty slots stay visible. Your stall uses the same tracker in small strips. At the start of buying, a lorry drives in along the street, customers shuffle aside, and crates drop onto the board; it drives off when buying ends, before selling.
- **Rationale:** Owner's explicit instruction. The tracker is the game's existing decay rule made visible, and the lorry turns the daily delivery into a moment.
- **Player impact:** Easier to see what is fresh and what is about to spoil; a lively start to each day.
- **Technical impact:** Visual only: the tracks follow src/engine/data.ts exactly; no rule or balance change.
- **Data and security:** None.
- **Cost:** RM0
- **Owner:** Murshidi
- **Approval:** Approved: “change the board layout to a vertical layout arranging the fruits from left to right instead, revert back to the decay style tracker for the game board, at the start of the buying turn, animate a lorry offloading the fruit on to the board, the customers are to make space accordingly, the lorry would leave before the selling turn” (2026-10-27)
- **Delivery:** Implemented (PLAN-008)
- **Review trigger:** If the vertical board is too tall on small phones, or the lorry slows down play (keep it skippable).
- **Reports:** REP-021
