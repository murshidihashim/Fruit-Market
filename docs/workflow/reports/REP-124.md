## 1. What 1.24.1 changes for players

All of it is in PLAN-107. The rules stay version 19, so games in progress carry on and nobody is asked to update.

- **The due day of a standing order.**
  - When you hold an order, your stocking rows now say how many pieces it takes, e.g. "3 for The Beach Resort".
  - Auto load puts those pieces in first.
  - The big button no longer says "Sell nothing today" while the order can be delivered. With an empty crate it says "Deliver nothing today · $6 stake lost".
  - At the bell a note says the order pays its own price, whatever price you set.
- **Undo and favour cards.**
  - A favour card played while buying (Scout, Fridge, Free Fruit) stays played.
  - Undo then takes back only what you buy after it. The button reads "↶ after Scout".
  - The card warns you before you play it.
  - This is the same alone and online.
- **Online:**
  - A wrong or closed room code says "That room doesn't exist or has closed. Check the code." with a Back button, instead of "Connecting…" for ever.
  - An invite link is used once: Create a room after it makes a new room.
  - A rematch starts when everyone still there agrees. A computer plays for anyone who left, and they can take their seat back. The results say "Benny has left."
  - In a private lobby, a player who closed the app is let go after about a minute. Until then the host's button says "Waiting for Gus to come back".
  - Invite links shared from the Android app now point at the game's web address instead of the phone itself.
- **Quick matches keep their upgrades**, as they always played. RULES.md now says so (ON-01).

## 3. Phones still on 1.24.0

The room server is shared, so the new server must work with old phones:
- **Wrong or old code:** they now see "Couldn't join · That room doesn't exist or has closed. Check the code." and stop trying.
- **Rematch after someone left:** they are not told who left, but the rematch starts once everyone still there agrees, them included.
- **Lobby with a closed tab:** the host sees "Waiting for everyone to be ready…" with no countdown. After about 75 seconds the seat goes and Start works.
- **Undo after a favour card online:** their Undo stays lit. The room answers "Nothing to take back", or takes back only what was bought after the card.
