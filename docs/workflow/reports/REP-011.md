## 2 · What option C means in practice
Written into `docs/workflow/CREATIVE-BIBLE.md` (short, one page):

  become the harbour; a port that trades with everywhere explains the mix of fruit.

  the rules for future content.

Rocky (raccoon), Pinky (pig). The panda's lines are calm and sleepy ("Bamboo-zled you!", "very zen",
"Nap time, I guess"). The new name should fit that pattern and keep the lines working.

## 4 · PLAN-006 · Rename the panda rival (cosmetic)
**Scope**
- Change the display name in the rival list (`src/engine/data.ts`) to your chosen name. Picture, lines,
  difficulty and play stay the same.
- Update the daily-challenge sentence in `RULES.md`.
- Keep "Panda Lim" reserved, so no player can take the old rival name.

**Acceptance**

- N3. The daily-challenge score check is unchanged for the same moves.
- N4. Both names are refused as player names.
- N5. `verify:full` is green in CI; no rule, save-format or server-logic changes.

**When:** built on a separate branch now and released as **1.1.2 after 1.1.1 is live**, so the 1.1.1
candidate you're testing doesn't change.

**Cost:** RM0. **Risk:** very low (one name plus tests). **Rollback:** revert the commit.
