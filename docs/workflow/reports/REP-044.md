## SUMMARY

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

**Why it was 1 vs 1.** DEC-016 (2026-10-26) set "one computer shop" to keep a first game short and the cause of a lost sale easy to read. It was a test-design choice, not a rule. The app supports 1 to 3 rival shops (new-game screen, `src/ui/App.tsx`), each set to Computer or Friend.

**Trade-off of 1 vs 3 (the owner chose 1 vs 3).** More like the intended 3 to 4 player game and the tabletop edition; more demand pressure and more to read on screen, so a lost sale may be harder to explain. Four shops also change supply (farmer cards add 1 of each fruit) and walk-ins per day.

**New tester questions (live at the form link)**
1. Did you finish the game? Yes, on my own / Yes, but I needed help / No, I stopped early
2. Day 3: a customer didn't buy from you. Do you know why? Yes, I knew exactly why / I had a rough idea / No idea
3. (optional) If you knew or had an idea, what was the reason?
4. How easy was it to understand what to do? Very easy / Okay / Confusing
5. Would you play again? Yes / Maybe / No
6. (optional) What was the most confusing moment, if any? (the day or screen)
7. (optional) Anything broken or hard to read? Your phone and browser, if you know.

**How they map to the locked criteria**
- Explain a lost sale by day 3: question 2 says Yes or rough idea AND the reason in question 3 is a correct one (judged against what the game shows). "No idea", or a wrong reason, counts as cannot explain.
- Finish the first game without help: question 1, "Yes, on my own".
- Play again: question 5 (at least half "Yes", with "Maybe" not counted as yes).
- Questions 4, 6 and 7 are supporting evidence only.

**Setup shown to testers:** New game, Rival shops 3 (all Computer), Quick, Open the shops. Other settings left as they are.

## RISKS AND COSTS

Tap answers are easier but give less detail. The reason question (3) carries the evidence for the main criterion and is optional, so some "Yes" answers will have no reason and will not count as explained. Three computers may undercut little (REP-036), which could make the game feel easier. Unobserved remote play remains weaker evidence than watching. Hand-played ageing (DEC-027) is untested. Cost: RM0 (Tally free plan, fair use unverified).
