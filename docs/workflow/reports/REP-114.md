## 1. The proposal

| Order | Highest bid | Stake |
|---|---|---|
| Blueberry | $9 → $11 | $6 → $4 |
| Watermelon | $12 → $14 | $8 → $6 |
| Apple | $11 → $13 | $7 → $5 |
| Mango | $18 → $20 | $12 → $10 |

Measured with the rules 18 balance scripts on the same seeds as REP-113 (patch `orderPlus2`): 1,500 games each at 3, 4 and 6 stalls with Normal computers, 1,000 at 4 stalls with Hard computers, and the paired test of bidding styles at 3 and 4 stalls (600 games a row).

## 2. Mango orders: a little better, not fixed

| | 3 stalls | 4 stalls | 6 stalls | 4 stalls, Hard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mango orders nobody bids for, now → proposed | 44% → 38% | 56% → 52% | 49% → 45% | 55% → 50% |
| Mango orders delivered in full | 65% → 67% | 51% → 56% | 39% → 39% | 66% → 66% |
| Stake lost for each mango order held | $3.71 → $2.95 | $5.01 → $3.80 | $6.29 → $5.11 | $3.75 → $3.10 |

The cause is untouched: a mango order still needs the stake and the Green mangoes on the same day, and Green mangoes are scarce. $2 off the stake moves about one order in twenty.

## 3. The other orders: the higher top bid does almost nothing

Rivals' bids set the price, not the top bid. At 4 stalls the winning watermelon bid is $7.82 now and $7.79 with the proposal; blueberry $6.29 and $6.25; apple $7.44 and $7.41. Orders with no bidder fall a little (all orders at 4 stalls: 17% → 16%). Stake lost for each order held falls (all orders at 4 stalls: $1.79 → $1.27).

## 4. What bidding is worth, and balance

| Seat made to bid one way, against bidding as the computer does | 3 stalls now → proposed | 4 stalls now → proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Always passes | −$1.57 → −$2.34 | −$0.88 → −$1.66 |
| Always bids the highest bid | −$0.93 → −$0.17 | −$0.80 → −$0.64 |
| Always bids 60% of the highest bid | −$6.58 → −$3.47 | −$3.84 → −$2.03 |
| Always bids half | −$10.20 → −$4.95 | −$7.95 → −$1.45 |
| Always bids $1 | −$28.92 → −$29.04 | −$31.78 → −$30.34 |

- Careful bidding becomes worth a little more ($1.70 to $2.30 a game over never bidding, from $0.90 to $1.60).
- Low bids are punished much less: a stall that always bids half the highest bid loses $1.45 a game at 4 stalls instead of $7.95. The stake stops reckless bids less well.
- **Shopkeepers:** no clear change. The largest distance from a fair win rate moves 3.6 → 5.1 points at 3 stalls, 4.6 → 2.9 at 4, 4.6 → 5.0 at 6 and 3.9 → 5.7 with Hard computers, each within about two standard errors (1.1 to 2.0 points a shopkeeper). Nest Egg and Bargain Hunter stay the strongest; Haggler and Shade Cloth the weakest.

## 5. Recommendation and options

The proposal makes orders a little more forgiving and a little more worth taking, but it does not fix mango orders, and it weakens the stake as a guard against wild bids. If the aim is mango orders, a different lever is needed. Options for the owner:

1. **Keep today's numbers** and add the sums on the bid sheet (REP-113 option 2, no rule change).
2. **Take the proposal as it stands** (rules version 19): small gain, mango still about half unbid.
3. **Fix mango orders directly** (rules version 19), each to be measured before choosing:
   a. a mango order is due in 3 days, so the mangoes can be bought the day after winning;
   b. a mango order wants one piece fewer (2, 3 and 4 by table size);
   c. replace the two mango order cards with one watermelon and one apple card.
4. **Combine**: the stake −$2 only (leave the highest bid), with one of 3a to 3c for mango.
