## 1. PLAN-081: flow repairs and two defects

Built by a helper in a separate work folder, then checked and merged by me (merge `f50c9d5`). Every finding from the flow study was confirmed in a running game before it was fixed; none was wrong.

| Item | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced motion | The open crates showed for 0.15 s and "No sales today" for 0.06 s | 2.9 s and 1.1 s, as with motion on; a tap still ends the pause |
| "Skip" in the rush | A tap on Skip only moved to the next customer | Skip reaches the day's takings |
| Hurry | Three rivals took 13 s to buy and a tap changed nothing | 12 s untouched, under 4 s after one tap on the shelf or the lanes |
| Market Day bid | The customer row emptied; the panel said "Bid in" before you bid | The row stays; the panel says the other stalls bid first until it is your turn |
| Night | With "Fast rivals" the panel closed before the slide ended | One clock: the slide always finishes |
| Lorry | A 0.7 s wait for a lorry that is not drawn | The fruit leaves at once |
| Solo browser test | It could get stuck at the Favoured Trade step without failing | It plays the step and fails if the results are not reached |
| A paused game | The Daily market, the Market League and New game replaced it without asking | A question first: "Keep my game" or "Start anyway" |
| Trophy names | Names with a space were dropped at every account sync | Kept. Progress already lost cannot be restored |

Two things to know:
- A tap's hurry does not carry into the rush. Otherwise a tap made while rivals set prices would skip the bell and every customer card.
- With the customer row kept, the bid panel needs a scroll to reach the price and Pass on phones 640 to 699 px tall. That panel is redrawn in package 3.

## 2. PLAN-082: the two rule changes

- Ah Chen (the Charmer) starts with 3 favour cards. She still holds up to 4 and earns up to 2 a day.
- The Ripening Room costs $4 and ripens green mangoes in one night. It no longer makes them $1 cheaper.
- Computer players now value the room by a fresh measurement. With no rival holding one it adds $1.54 a game for a Normal computer (800 games) and $2.81 for a Hard one (600 games). It was about $7.70 with the discount.
- The rules version stays 18: it has not shipped and is not on the preview.
- One existing test was changed on purpose. The check that the pricing hint "Enough buyers" agrees with the bell did not allow for pieces a stall delivers to its own standing order. The new numbers changed which seeded games it plays and exposed that. The hint was right; the test was corrected.
