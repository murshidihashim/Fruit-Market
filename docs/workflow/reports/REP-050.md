## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

**How I read the message.** "else do plan a + c" = go with A + C. "target is so that the board would not be empty" = the target is that shops can always buy something, measured as the share of shop turns that find an empty market. "Why the number in decimal" is answered below.

**Why the numbers had decimals.** They are averages over hundreds of simulated games. A single game always has whole numbers (for example 5 customers on a day); the average of 300 games is 5.3. The percentages are shares of shop turns.

**Changes**
- `src/engine/game.ts`: the lorry card is drawn every day; walk-ins are players minus 2, plus 1 on a Market Day, plus any event.
- `src/engine/ai.ts`: computer shops no longer treat a Market Day as a no-lorry shortage (one fixed weight for scarce fruit).
- `src/net/protocol.ts`: rules version 5 (old saves will not resume; daily boards are kept per version; online players need the same version).
- Screens: the lorry shows on Market Days, the strip says "Market Day: one more walk-in", and How to play, set-up and hint text are updated.
- `RULES.md` (tabletop master) updated: lorry every day, walk-ins every day, version 5, balance note.
- Tests updated: walk-ins, Market Day delivery, demand staying level, version 5.

**Tabletop check (DEC-027).** The farmer card is flipped every day, as it already was on other days. Walk-ins are a printed number: players minus 2. Nothing new to move or count.
