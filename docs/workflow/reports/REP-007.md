## 1 · What you noticed
You couldn't see an option for 4 human players. 4-player games are supported, but the screens don't say so.

**Online room (Play a friend)**, as it looks today on a 360 px phone:
- The heading says **"Invite a friend"** (singular).
- There is one empty row: **"Waiting for a friend to join"**, which disappears once one person joins.
- The count "1/4" is in small grey text next to "Players".

**Same phone (New game):** 4 people means choosing **3** rival shops and switching each from
Computer to Friend. Nothing on the screen explains that.

**Quick match** is always 2 strangers, by design. Nothing points groups to Play a friend instead.

## 2 · Check that 4 players really work (read-only, local server)
- 4 browsers joined one room by the same link. The lobby showed "Players 4/4". All 4 pressed ready, the
  host started, and **all 4 reached the game** (each saw 3 rivals).
- A **5th** person was turned away: "Couldn't join · This room is full."
- Limitation: tested on one machine, not 4 real phones. The automated test below makes this a permanent check.

## 3 · PLAN-005 · 4-player clarity

**Player problem:** groups of 3 or 4 think the game is for 2 and give up, or play with computers instead.

**Included scope (screen text and layout only)**

| Screen | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Online lobby, invite box | "Invite a friend" | **"Invite friends (up to 3)"** |
| Online lobby, player list | "Players 1/4" (small, grey), one row "Waiting for a friend to join" | **"Players 1 of 4"** in normal text, plus one row per free seat: "Open seat · send the link" |
| Online lobby, when full | (nothing) | "Room full · start when everyone is ready" |
| Home, under "Play a friend" | (nothing) | Small line: "2 to 4 players online" |
| Quick match screen | (nothing) | "Quick match is always 2 players. For 3 or 4, use Play a friend." |
| New game, under "Rival shops" | (nothing) | "Up to 4 shops. Pick Friend to pass this phone around." plus a live total, for example "4 shops: you, 2 friends, 1 computer" |

Computer shops added in the lobby take seats, so the open-seat rows and the count include them. For
example: 2 people + 1 computer = "Players 3 of 4", one open seat.

**Also included:** a new automated browser test where 4 people join one room and all reach the
game, and a 5th is refused. It runs in CI on every change.

**Excluded**
- Rules, balance, the 4-player limit and quick-match size.
- Any server or message change.
- Renaming the buttons people tap to get there ("Play a friend", "New game"), so tests and habits keep working.
- Publishing.

**Roles:** UX designer (wording), gameplay engineer, QA. Self-review; low risk.

**Likely files:** `src/ui/Online.tsx` (lobby), `src/ui/App.tsx` (home, new game), `src/ui/styles.css`,
new `scripts/e2e-four.cjs`, `scripts/e2e.mjs`.

**Acceptance (observable)**
- C1. The lobby shows "Players N of 4" and one open-seat row per free seat. Both update when people join or leave and when computer shops are added.
- C2. With 4 people there are no open seats and the lobby says the room is full.
- C3. The new browser test passes: 4 people join one room, all see the game, and a 5th is refused.
- C4. The new lines fit on a 360 px wide phone without overflow (screenshots of each screen in the report).
- C5. Store screenshots change only where intended (home and lobby text), checked with the visual-diff tool.
- C6. `verify:full` is green in CI; no rules, server or save changes.

**Cost and data:** RM0. No data changes.

**Risks:** longer text could wrap awkwardly on small phones; C4 checks this. Wording is a matter of
taste, so you can change any line before or after.

**Rollback:** revert the package commit.

**After it's built:** open the preview, tap Play a friend → Create room, and check that the lobby
clearly invites up to 3 friends.
