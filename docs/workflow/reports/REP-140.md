Three read-only audits ran in parallel on local copies of the live code. The live and preview sites were not used.
- **A:** code readability.
- **B:** screens and ease of use.
- **C:** rules engine and every mode.

The evidence (screenshots, logs, test probes) is in the session scratchpad, `audit140/A`, `B` and `C`.

## 1. In short

- **The rules engine works as the rulebook says.**
  - 600 random games, 79 targeted checks and the rulebook's own example all PASS.
  - No game got stuck.
  - No secret (prices, crates, bids, favour cards) leaked to another player.
- **Every mode reached its results:**
  - Computer (2 to 6 stalls, 8 to 14 days, Easy to Hard);
  - Pass & Play;
  - Quick Match at 2, 4 and 6 stalls;
  - Private Room;
  - Public rooms;
  - League (all 14 stages open validly);
  - Daily, whose leaderboard replay check accepted the scores;
  - Sprint (with retry).
- **No Blocker.** Two things are wrong for players (Major), one item is a balance question for the owner, and the rest is polish and code health.

## 3. Accessibility (Minor)

- **B-08 · Keyboard focus:** there is no visible focus ring inside the game (Menu, the event chip, Start buying, the market lanes).
- **B-09 · Customer cards:** they are not buttons, so they can't be opened with the keyboard, and the regular customer has no name for screen readers.
- **B-10 · Small text:** some labels are 8 to 9 px:
  - set-up seat labels;
  - the order numbers and "in crate" on the table;
  - "wanted" and "Lasts until night".
- **B-11 · Contrast too low:**
  - the Rush's faded "why not sold" captions (1.0 to 2.4:1);
  - the red "Online play isn't available" warning and the Market Day "Got it" button (4.0:1);
  - the market counts when it isn't your turn (2.7:1).

## 4. Smaller polish (B)

- **B-16:** How to play's chapter tab scrolls out of view.
- **B-17:** long names are cut ("UNCLE …"; online names cut to 14 letters).
- **B-18:** Share gives no sign it worked on a computer.
- **B-19:** the same thing has two names ("Stall" and "Seat"; "Market" and "Home").
- **B-20:** there are extra taps:
  - Continue opens a pop-up that needs Resume;
  - the Custom card's four ways are repeated on the Play page.
- **B-21:** on a computer screen, the set-up pages stretch to about 1100 px.
- **C-05:** a save from before version 1.2 continues under today's rules instead of showing the "older rules" notice. Very few phones will have one.
- **C-06:** the rulebook's opening example simplifies Bobby Books' card (he also wants a mango).
