## 1. What was looked at

The live game was walked in a browser at 390x844 (phone, upright), 844x390 (phone, sideways) and 1280x800:
Home with each of the four modes, the Play page and each of its four ways, the League, Daily and Sprint pages, the Stall and Rules tabs.

Seen on Home:
- The four ways to play a Custom match are on Home's card and again on the Play page, with different lines under them. Each way has two routes.
- The Daily and Sprint cards have large empty gaps; the League card is full.
- Modes change only by tapping the four tabs. A swipe on the card does nothing.
- The shopkeepers are small circles (36 to 44 px). Their stall pictures show only on set-up pages.
- Nothing on Home moves after the opening.

## 2. The proposal

- **Left, about 40% of the width: the street.**
  - Your own stall on top. It takes the place of the profile chip; a tap opens the Stall tab.
  - Under it, the shopkeepers you would face in the card showing: three rivals for Custom, Rosa for League stage 1, today's rivals for the Daily and the Sprint.
  - They bob, walk in when the card changes, and say their real lines from the game in a bubble. A tap makes one speak.
- **Right, about 60%: one card per mode.**
  - Swipe, or tap a name under the cards. The next card peeks in.
  - Each card says what the mode is and where you stand: the League stage and stars, today's first customer and lorry, your next goal.
- **One Play button** across the foot for the card showing. It becomes Continue, with New game beside it, when that mode has a game waiting.
- The fruit lanes, the title, Settings and the bottom bar (Stall, Market, Rules) stay.

## 3. Where each button leads

One rule for every card: the card tells you about the mode, Play opens that mode's page, the choices are made there.

| Button | Leads to | Against today |
|---|---|---|
| Custom match, Play | The Play page: Quick Match, Private Room, Computer, Pass & Play; each then opens its own page | Changed: the four buttons leave Home's card |
| Market League, Play | The League page, then Start | Same |
| Daily market, Play | The Daily page, then Start | Same |
| Sprint, Play | The Sprint page, then Start | Same |
| Continue | Straight back into the game | Same |
| New game | Asks first, then where Play goes | Same |
| Your stall (left, top) | The Stall tab | Changed: replaces the profile chip |
| A shopkeeper | They say a line; no page opens | New |
| Gear, bottom bar | Settings; Stall, Market, Rules | Same |

## 4. Limits and costs

- The phone is upright, so the left side is narrow (about 150 of 390 px). Cards carry shorter text than today; the detail stays on each mode's page.
- The shopkeeper pictures are single drawings. They can bob, nod, walk in and speak in bubbles with no new art. Waving arms or blinking needs new drawings.
- Quick Match goes from one tap to two.
- The Reduced motion setting would stop the movement.
- The mockup is a picture of the idea. It was looked at once at phone size and once on a wide screen. No size below 390 px wide was tried, and no game test applies to it.
- No money cost. No new dependency is expected.

## 5. For the owner to decide

1. Is the split right on an upright phone (left 40%, right 60%)? The other choice is characters across the top with wider cards under them.
2. May the four buttons leave the Custom card, so Play always opens the Play page?
3. Is moving the existing pictures enough for a first version, or should new drawings be planned as their own work?

After the answers, the next step is a plan for approval. Nothing is built before that.
