## 1. What changed in the rules (PLAN-069, DEC-079)

- **Fruit lives 3, 4, 5, 6.** Blueberry 3 days, watermelon 4, mango 5, apple 6. One rule for all: the last two days are Okay then Soft, every day before is Fresh. Every mango arrives green ($3) and ripens for two nights.
- **A row of five.** One customer per stall, never more than five; with six stalls each customer wants one more piece. Nobody waits overnight. A guest or a standing order takes the last walk-in's place. Market Days no longer add walk-ins.
- **Supply and demand.** Twenty lorries rescaled (about 8, 11, 15, 19 and 24 pieces for 2 to 6 stalls). A new deck of 36 customers with one or two fruits each. Usual prices: blueberry $5, watermelon $7, apple $6, mango $11; Bargain $1 or $2 under, Premium $1 or $2 over.
- **Standing orders.** Eight cards. Turned over on a Market Day morning; every stall secretly bids a price per piece, or passes. Lowest bid wins (ties: Order Book, then earlier in the buying order). Two days later the order stands first in the row, takes the winner's Fresh or Okay pieces at its bid, then rivals' pieces at the full card price. The winner is fined for every piece it did not supply.
- **Upgrades.** Juice Bar $4, Handcart $6 (hold 10), Premium Display $6, Ripening Room $6 (new), Order Book $5 (new). Two to a stall. Shelf holds 8.
- **Words.** "Tired" is "Soft" everywhere on screen.
- RULES_VERSION 13 to 14; computer-player version 2 to 3. Festival and Tourist Bus cards now say "up to a row of five".

Not built: face-down crates and bluffing (researched in the project note "engine revamp research").

## 2. What changed in the look (PLAN-070, DEC-080)

- Colours, type (Archivo, Archivo Black) and flat fruit are the same as the print-and-play kit.
- 81 flat pictures (customers, guests, shopkeepers, events, standing orders) in `public/art-ticket`. The owner's first answer was "Keep my pictures"; the later request for the new art style replaced it. His pictures are still in `public/art`, unused.
- Game screen: black awning bar, yellow street, cash as a price tag, kraft footer, red action button.
- Recoloured but not redrawn: card frames and backs, crates, the lorry, the home street. Worn looks were made for the old portraits.

## 3. The night (PLAN-071)

After Lights out a panel shows the Wholesale Market and your shelf, in the kit's lane order. Each piece slides one box older; Soft pieces slide off. A Heatwave slides two boxes, a Monsoon none, a Shade Cloth fruit stays.

## 6. Risks

- Balance is untuned: Local Favourite and Tip Jar are weak, Charmer strong; Premium Display and Order Book are poor first buys.
- Days with no sale for a stall rose from 5-9% to 11-21%.
- Computer players do not deliberately sabotage a rival's order, so the fine bites less against them than against people.
- Unfinished rules 13 games cannot be resumed; the daily board starts fresh; older apps must update to play online.
- The look is a mix: new pictures and colours on the old card frames and crates.
