## 1. What was made

Two PDFs in `print/tracker-test`, built by `scripts/print-trackers.ts` from the game data (fruit lives and costs come from `src/engine/data.ts`). Page 1 of each is the how-to, turn by turn.

| File | Pages | What to print |
|---|---|---|
| `Fruit-Market-Tracker-Test-1-Ruler.pdf` | 4 | How-to (A4). Stall sheet, one for each stall (A4 landscape). Market board (A4 landscape). Rulers to cut out, eight to the page: one for each stall and one for the market |
| `Fruit-Market-Tracker-Test-2-Tray.pdf` | 3 | How-to (A4). Stall sheet, one for each stall (A4). Crate trays to cut out, two to the page: print it three times for six stalls |

Everything else comes from the existing printing: fruit pieces (15 mm), shutters, price tags, counters, cards. Tracker 2 keeps the market mat; tracker 1 replaces its fruit boxes with the market board.

**Tracker 1, the ruler.** Sixteen numbered columns (a Long game is 14 days, plus one for a Heatwave night, plus a spare). Every box carries its column number. Fruit is bought into the same column number it left on the market board and never moves to age. A loose ruler (99 mm by 29.5 mm) shows, for each fruit, the letter over each of the last six columns and a cross where fruit is spoiled. Two shutter lines: in front of the crate rows until the bell, behind them at the bell.

**Tracker 2, the tray.** Shelf boxes by freshness, newest on the right, with the letter on a strip above each box so a fruit piece does not cover it. The crate is a loose tray (174 mm by about 100 mm) with the same boxes and a place for each price tag; it lies on a bay in front of the shelf and comes out whole at the bell.

Both how-to pages say the same about unsold fruit: it stays where it is (in the crate row, or on the tray) and tomorrow's Stocking may leave it or slide it back. They also cover Heatwave, Monsoon Downpour, Shade Cloth, the Fridge and the Health Inspector.

## 3. How to try them

Play three or four days with each, with at least three stalls, and include a Heatwave or a Monsoon Downpour if one turns up. Count: fruit put in the wrong place, missed or doubled night moves, and how long the night takes. Then tell me 1 or 2 and the print kit is built with it.

## 4. What comes next, and the app's screens

Nothing more is needed from the owner before the screen work starts. Settled already: the Market Ticket look; the printed card as the layout the app's cards follow; the plain voice with a Malaysian market feel (DEC-087); Stocking skipped with nothing ripe, evenings only with a choice, one card per customer in the rush; today's buys on show; the live defects to fix; the trophies; the new screens the rules need (the stake, the $5 top-up, the Favoured Trade question).

Decided on the mock-ups, not before: the one card design at phone size (customer, event, favour, upgrade, shopkeeper, lorry, standing order); which further memory aids to show (buying order, days until a mango ripens, what you paid, how fresh your crate is while pricing, yesterday's prices); the restyled Results, Settings, Trophies, Wardrobe and How to play.

Order of work: (1) the rules 18 build comes back, is checked and tested; (2) screen and card mock-ups for approval, which can be drawn while the build finishes; (3) the text rewrite; (4) the screens built to the approved mock-ups; (5) the trophies; (6) the whole print kit, with the tracker chosen after the table test; (7) one release, with separate approval to publish.
