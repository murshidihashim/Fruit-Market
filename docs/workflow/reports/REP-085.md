## SUMMARY

- **Accounts:** an ID (3 to 20 letters, digits or underscore) and a password (8 to 72 characters). No email and no password recovery. Progress syncs and only grows. Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, sessions as hashed tokens, and 5 wrong tries lock the ID for a growing time. Signing out clears the progress on that phone after syncing. Deleting the account needs the password.
- **Achievements:** 43, in ten themes. Every stall colour is free.
- **Looks:** 25 pieces unlocked by achievements. Stall roofs, trims and counter props show on the stall; hats, faces, neck pieces and badges are worn by the shopkeeper. They change no rule.
- **Online:** each player's look is sent to the room and shown on their seat, stall, showdown and results. The Wardrobe opens from the room lobby and the pause menu. Protocol 3: older apps cannot join new rooms.

## KNOWN LIMITS

- The server checks that a look exists and fits its slot, not that the player earned it. A modified phone could wear a look it has not unlocked. It is cosmetic only.
- There is no way to recover a lost password.
- Owner still to do: update the Google Play data-safety form and add a privacy-policy link (accounts store an ID, a password hash and progress).
