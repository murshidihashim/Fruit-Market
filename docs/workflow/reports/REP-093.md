## 1. Event card wording

- All 20 event cards use the owner's wording, split into a tagline ("Big crowds, bigger spending!") and the rule ("Customers pay $1 more per fruit. Add 1 walk-in (2 at 4–6 stalls).").
- The tagline shows in red italics on the morning card and in bold at the start of the newspaper card's story. The rule book table and the print kit use the same wording.
- **Changed from the owner's text:** the Heatwave line "Cold Rooms prevent this" and the Health Inspector line "Cold Rooms won't help!" are left out, because the Cold Room was removed in the same package (DEC-078). The rules themselves did not change; every number matches the game.

## 2. Redrawn artwork (proposal)

The first drafts (REP-092) were rejected: thin lines, cool colours and small detail did not match the owner's 13 event pictures. The seven were redrawn on the same recipe as the existing art:

- a wide two-tone sunburst and a faded skyline behind;
- big central objects with thick, slightly rough navy outlines;
- dotted halftone shading on the lower right and white shine marks;
- rosy-cheeked people cropped at the bottom edge;
- cream price tags with a hole, and paper grain;
- colours sampled from the existing pictures.

Each scene follows the picture caption the game already prints on the newspaper card (for example "A spiky durian on a throne, shoppers holding their noses"). The files are in `docs/studio/art-drafts/events/` (520 x 391 WebP, like the owner's art). They are not in the game yet; those cards still say "Art coming". The preview of all 20 cards (private artifact, link in chat) shows them on the morning screen.
