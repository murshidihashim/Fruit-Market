## 1. What changed

- Before: one picture of the fruit, then a dot for each piece. Two pieces looked like three things.
- Now: a picture of the fruit for each piece, and nothing else. Three apples wanted is three apples drawn.
- Where: the customer tickets on the game table, the whole customer card (opened by a tap), and the larger customer card on the table.
- A ticket too narrow for its pictures shows one picture and "×3", as it showed a number before.
- Pictures: `img/REP-156-tickets.png` (live above, new below) and `img/REP-156-card.png`.

## 2. A fault found in live 1.33.0, fixed here

- On Home, after changing tab or swiping, a first customer's card opened by a tap stayed inside the mode card's box, and Rosa's shoes showed over it.
- Cause: the card's slide-in kept its effect after it ended, which holds a pop-up to the card's box.
- It was missed in PLAN-132: the checks opened no card after a tab change.
- Fixed: the pop-up fills the screen (390 x 844 measured) and covers Rosa.

## 3. Not changed

- Standing orders keep their dots. They ask for more pieces, and were not part of the request.
- What customers want and pay; rules, balance, saves.
- The printed deck uses the same customer card, so a future print would show the pictures too. Nothing is reprinted.
