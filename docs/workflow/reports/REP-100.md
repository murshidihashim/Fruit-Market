## 1. Rule changes in the app (rules version 17)

| Rule | Before (rules 16) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| A rival fills a standing order's gap | Paid the card's full price (e.g. $18 a mango) | Paid **$1 under the winner's bid**, never under $1 |
| Bidding | Every stall bid in secret, once | Everyone sees who is **bidding**, who **passed** and who is **deciding**; never a price. A stall may change its bid or pass while another stall is still deciding. The last stall to decide closes the bidding |
| Computer stalls | Bid after the person | Decide first, so the person sees them |
| Upgrades after a Market Day | Three of the unbuilt ones, at random | **Every** unbuilt upgrade (all five to start with) |
| What an order's ticket shows | The card's price (+$1 on Closing Day) | The winner's bid; no event or Closing Day changes it |

Online, the server tells each phone only the statuses and that phone's own bid. `RULES_VERSION` 16 to 17 and computer players 4 to 5: unfinished games cannot be resumed, the daily board starts fresh, older apps must update to play online.

One thing to watch: a standing order takes rivals' crate pieces whether they like it or not. With a low winning bid a rival can now lose a piece for $1 or $2 that a customer would have paid more for.

## 3. The tabletop update pack (`print/update-17`)

Built by `scripts/print-update.ts` from the game data, so it can be rebuilt: `npx vite-node scripts/print-update.ts`.

| File | Pages | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `Fruit-Market-Rules-Guidebook-v17.pdf` | 10 | The whole guidebook at rules 17 (text and tables; the old pictures of the board and cards are not redrawn) |
| `Fruit-Market-Update-17-1-Stall-Sheets-and-Counters.pdf` | 13 | A change sheet with cut-out notes for the board; six stall sheets without a cash track; six counters, one to a landscape page, each with that stall's cash track |
| `Fruit-Market-Update-17-2-Standing-Order-Cards.pdf` | 2 | The eight standing-order cards (front and back), 63 x 88 mm |

The board, shutters, fruit pieces, tags, markers and all other cards are kept. On the table, bidding in the open is: a tag face down in front of your shutter (bidding) or "pass"; change it until the last stall decides; then flip.

Boxes are 17 mm for the 15 mm fruit tiles; cash cells are 13 mm. These sizes are new and have not been printed.
