## 3 · Playtest kit (DEC-016)
**Goal:** find out whether first-time players understand *why* they won or lost a sale.

**Before:** use the live game after 1.1.1 is published (or the preview). Each tester uses their own
phone. Don't explain the rules. You only watch and take notes.

**Say only this:** "This is a game about running a fruit stall. Please think aloud while you play. I
can't help, but I'll ask a few questions at the end."

**Tasks:** New game → Quick (8 days) → 1 computer shop. Play to the end.

**Note for each tester** (copy this per person):
```
Tester:            Age range:        Plays phone games? (often / sometimes / rarely)
Minutes to first price set:
After day 1–3 sales, in their words: why did they sell or not sell?
Did they mention "cheapest sells first" or fresher fruit? (yes / no)
Did they read the event card? (yes / no / unsure)
Where did they hesitate or get stuck?
Finished the game? (yes / no)   Started another game without being asked? (yes / no)
"What were you trying to do?"
"Why did you lose that customer?"
"Would you play again?"
Anything that made them laugh or frown:
```

**Decision rule (provisional, you set it):** **continue** if at least 3 of 4 testers can explain a lost
sale by day 3; **revise the reveal** (the chalkboard idea in REP-010) if 2 or fewer can.

**Send me:** the notes, as typed text or photos. I'll record what you *saw* separately from what we
*think* it means, and propose the smallest next step.
