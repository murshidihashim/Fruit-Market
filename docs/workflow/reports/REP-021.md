## What's on the Buying screen now
- **Market as a decay tracker, vertical:** four columns left to right (blueberry, watermelon, apple,
  mango). New and ripening fruit at the top, spoiling at the bottom, with a colour bar (green, gold,
  brown). Every slot shows its price; empty slots stay as dashed outlines, like today's game.
  The tracks match the real rules: blueberry 3 slots, watermelon 5 + 1 unripe, apple 7, mango 4 + 2 unripe.
- **Your stall uses the same tracker** as four small strips; fruit you buy pops into its slot.
- **The lorry:** when buying starts it drives in along the street, Grandpa Tan and Cowboy John shuffle
  left to make room, and the crates drop one by one onto the board. Press **Done buying** and the lorry
  drives off, the customers spread back out, and the button changes to "Lorry's gone · set prices →".
  A small **↻ replay lorry** button lets you watch it again.
