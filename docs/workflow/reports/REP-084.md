## STATUS

Built. Lint, types and 135 unit tests pass locally. The new print pages were rendered and checked on screen, not printed on paper.

## SUMMARY

The review compared RULES.md, the game engine, the printed kit and 600 computer-played games (2 to 4 stalls, 8, 10 and 14 days).

Found and fixed:
- **Guest orders were not printed.** The Hotel Chef, Influencer and Gym Club orders and top prices are now in RULES.md and on three new guest cards.
- **Bad Harvest said "scarce fruit"** but raises every fruit by $2. **Fridge said "all fruit"** but moves ripe fruit only. Both texts now match the game, in the app and on the printed cards. The Hotel Order card now lists the order.
- **Rules wording added:** how a tie on "fresher fruit" is decided (the younger freshest piece wins), that customers are drawn a day ahead, that the crate is a number and the pieces stay on the shelf, that the first-player token moves one seat each night, how decks run out and are reshuffled, rounding for Good Harvest, the Health Inspector cap, full favour hands, money, and which customers are generous in the Fixed prices option.
- **The favour deck could deal a third copy of a card** after it ran out, which a real deck cannot do. It ran out in about 1 game in 6. The game now shuffles back only the played cards. A new test fails on the old code and passes on the new.
- **Print kit:** a new Table_Extras pack (guest cards, stall sheet with crate numbers and price row, fold-up screen with reminders, and paper money for a $910 bank). Event cards and the playtest pack updated. Parts check: the most of one fruit ever in play was 25 against 30 tokens.

## NOT DONE OR NOT VERIFIED

Nothing was printed or played on a table. The two ways of tracking age (sliding tokens on the printed mat, or day chips) are both described and both give the same ages; choosing one is left to the first table test. The age slots on the stall sheet use the sliding method. Token counts come from computer players only.
