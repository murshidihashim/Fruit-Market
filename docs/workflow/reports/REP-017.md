## The idea in one line
The market is sunny by day, but **pricing is a secret game**. When something is secret, the screen turns
**plum and gold**, rivals look sly, and the reveal plays like a showdown. Cheeky, never mean.

## Fit with the approved pillars
- **Pillar 2, outprice rivals in secret:** now the strongest visual idea.
- **Pillar 3, a cheerful town market:** kept. Rivals tease and wink; nobody is cruel; daytime stays sunny.
  If playtesters read the rivals as mean, we soften them (DEC-022 review trigger).
- **Privacy rule:** whispers only repeat what the game already shows (who holds what). They never reveal
  a rival's secret price or choice.

## Limits
- Mockups, not the game; numbers are examples. Characters other than Foxy Rosa, Patches, Grandpa Tan and
  Cowboy John are not drawn yet. No sound yet.
- Plum screens need a contrast check in the build (text sizes and colours were chosen for it, not measured).
