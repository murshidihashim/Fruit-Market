## 1. What is there today

- **Daily market:** one shared seed a day; the same three rivals (Rosa Normal, Uncle Benny Hard, Ah Chen Normal); a weekly twist (10, 8 or 14 days, no upgrades, and so on). The server replays every submitted game with its own rules and computers, so scores cannot be faked; each phone keeps its best score; the board holds the top 100.
- **Computer rivals** already use only what they could see (a walk-in's card only if they heard it; crates by count; their memory of rivals' shelves by level).
- **Market League:** six stages (Village Square to Royal Market) against computer rivals, each 8 or 10 days; win to move up.
- **Already in the card set:** the Pasar Malam event (the night hawker buys 1 of each fruit left in a crate for $3) and standing orders, such as the hotel's.

## 2. Proposal

### PLAN-114 · Market Sprint and its leaderboard (release 1.27.0)

- **Market Sprint, a second Daily format** beside today's Daily market:
  - Three days, four stalls, the day's shared seed, fixed shopkeepers and starting cash.
  - The same computer rivals as the Daily, no upgrades, and no standing orders (an order needs more days than a Sprint has to deliver).
- **The Daily card on Home shows both,** labelled "Sprint · 3 days" and "Daily market · {length}".
- **Score:** final cash, then fruit sold (the Daily's own order).
- **Target:** 5 to 8 minutes. The actual time is measured by playing it in the browser, and reported. If it runs long, the fix is fewer customers or less setup, never a rule change.
- **Results:**
  - your best today, and the place it gives you;
  - one concrete reason taken from the game, for example "Day 2: Uncle Benny's $4 sold first to Zara Zapp; yours was $6";
  - **Try again**, which replays the same Sprint at once.
- **Leaderboard** (part 2 of your list):
  - A Sprint board per day, beside the Daily board, kept and checked the same way (the server replays the moves). Retries are allowed; your best counts.
  - On results and in the Daily card: your place ("12th of 48"), the top 10, and the players just above and below you.
  - A weekly Sprint ranking that adds up your best Sprint score of each day.
- **What players will notice:** a short new format to play, retry and compare.
- **No change to:** rules, prices, cards, the Daily market itself or online play. The rules version stays 20. The Sprint is a new game set-up, so the server needs to know it: the online protocol does not change, but phones on 1.26.0 will not have the Sprint until they update.

### PLAN-115 · The Market League as a solo campaign (release 1.28.0)

- **Six short, authored stages,** each a fixed scenario with its own goal and rivals whose style reads clearly. The first three:
  1. **Village Square:** when to undercut. One rival always prices just under the top price; you learn to go one dollar under.
  2. **Pasar Malam:** the night hawker's $3 floor changes how low it is worth going on leftovers (the existing Pasar Malam card, set to come up on chosen days).
  3. **The Hotel Order:** bid low enough to win the standing order, high enough to profit, with enough days to deliver (the existing standing-order rules, on a set day).

  The other three follow the same pattern, built on cards already in the game: a heatwave, a glut, a fussy premium buyer.
- **Each stage has:**
  - a short illustrated moment with the real character art;
  - one goal to clear it;
  - three optional mastery goals (for example "sell every watermelon", "never price over the top", "win the order");
  - stamps and looks that show up on your stall through the Stamp Book, Collection and Wardrobe.
- **Underneath:** stages fix their seed and say which event comes on which day. That is a new set-up option in the game engine; the rules themselves do not change. Each stage is checked with the balance tools so a careful player can clear it.
- **What players will notice:** a short campaign that teaches the game's main decisions, with something to earn in every stage.

## 4. Risks and costs

- **Cost:** RM0 for the work. A second daily board adds a little storage on the existing Cloudflare plan. Capacity is still unmeasured (PLAN-003).
- **Balance:** a three-day game swings more on luck. The balance run will show whether one shopkeeper wins too often.
- **Effort:** two releases. The Sprint and its board come first, as asked.

## 5. Questions for the owner

1. **Retries:** may players retry the Sprint as often as they like, with their best counting on the board (my pick)? Or should only the first try count?
2. **The Daily card:** the Sprint beside the existing Daily market (my pick), or the Sprint replacing it?
3. **The campaign's extra stages:** should stages 4 to 6 use the heatwave, glut and premium-buyer ideas (my pick), or do you have other lessons in mind?
