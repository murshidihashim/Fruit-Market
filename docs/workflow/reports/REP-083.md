## STATUS

Built. Lint, types and 135 unit tests pass locally. CI runs the browser suite. A copy of the cards was checked on a phone-sized screen.

## SUMMARY

How to play is now seven cards in the order a game unfolds: **Welcome to Fruit Market** (what to expect, the $10 start, and the five stages of a game), **The seven phases of a day**, **Four fruits, four personalities**, **The rush: who gets the sale?**, **Evening and night**, **Market Days and Closing Day**, and **Tips from the market**. The first-game tips have a **Skip tutorial** button next to Got it. Settings can replay them.

Every tip was checked against the rules. One was wrong (it said later days get busier; customer numbers stay level) and was removed, and two more were removed at the owner's request.

## NOT DONE OR NOT VERIFIED

The Skip tutorial button has not been tried in a real game, and the final seven cards have not been looked at on a real phone. The shopkeepers and upgrades are no longer listed in How to play (the upgrades appear on the Market Days card).
