## A. How the game is won and lost (overview)

- **You win by having the most cash when the last day's sales end.** Nothing else counts: unsold fruit, upgrades and
  favour cards are worth $0 at the end (`ranking()` in `src/engine/game.ts`).
- **Nobody is ever knocked out.** There is no bankruptcy, no health, no timer and no early finish. Every game lasts
  exactly its length: 8, 10 or 14 days. "Losing" just means not finishing first.
- **Ties:** most cash wins; if level, most fruit sold; if still level, the **seat order decides silently**
  (seat 0, which is you in solo, daily and league games). The results screen then wrongly says it was "decided on fruit sold".
- **Where money comes from:** buying fruit cheaply at the farmers market and selling it to customers through
  **secret, sealed prices** where the **cheapest offer sells first**. Freshness limits the top price you may ask and
  breaks price ties. Everything else (upgrades, traits, favour cards, events) moves the margins.
- **What really decides games** (measured): pricing skill (Hard beats Normal 84% of the time head to head), buying
  only what customers want, the Signboard and Cool Room upgrades, and the event deck. Turn order is a deliberate
  catch-up: the poorest shop buys first.
- **Long-term goals outside a single game:** the 6-stage Market League (win to advance, unlimited retries),
  the daily challenge (score = final cash on a shared leaderboard), and 12 achievements.

**Order inside a day** (it matters for every outcome above): morning (event revealed; delivery added) → buying in turn
order → customers arrive → everyone seals prices → sales resolved customer by customer (yesterday's waiting customers
first, then today's) → evening upgrades (not on the last day) → night: inspector fine, then everything ages, then
the next day starts.

**Conflicting or unreachable outcomes found**
- On the **final day**, Health Inspector and Heatwave's extra ageing do nothing (they act at night, and there is no
  last night). Priority can't be played (no evening). These cards still appear as the final day's event.
- The **seat-order tie-break** favours seat 0 (you in solo, daily and league games, the room host online).
- `rich` ($200) is practically unreachable in 4-player games.

## D. Progression and pacing map

**Units:** money in $, time in game days. Computer-only measurements, 10-day games unless stated.

### Early game: days 1–3 ("set-up")
- Day 1 is thin: one forecast customer plus one walk-in, orders scaled to 40% of buying capacity.
  Average income per shop on day 1: **$6.3 (2p), $4.9 (4p)**, against $10 starting cash. Most shops spend nearly all
  their cash on day 1.
- Supply is about **2.5× new demand on day 1** (formula: supply 100% vs demand 40% of capacity). Unbought market fruit
  ages and becomes cheap, so not-so-fresh fruit at $1 is almost always available.
- First upgrades: computers buy the Signboard on day ~2.6, Handcart ~2.5–3.1, Cool Room ~2.8, Bell ~2.8–3.4. The AI only
  builds when it keeps ≥ $6 afterwards, so the $8 Signboard needs $14 in hand.
- Traits make the biggest difference here: Nest Egg's +$6 is about one full day's income at this point.

### Mid game: days 4–6
- Income climbs: 2p **$22–27 a day**, 4p **$13–16 a day**.
- Walk-ins double at the halfway day (day 6 of 10), so there are more but smaller orders, more showdowns and longer reveals.
- Juice Press is usually built here (day ~5) and pays nothing back for careful players.
- **Who's ahead on day 5 usually wins:** 66% (2p), 54% (3p), 47% (4p), against fair chances of 50/33/25%.

### Late game: days 7–10
- Income plateaus: 2p **$28–30 a day**, 4p **$16–19 a day** (demand reaches 85% of capacity; supply 150%).
- Upgrades stop (the computer needs 3–6 days left for each). Last day: no evening; unsold fruit is worth $0.
- Computers still buy a full basket (~4) on the last day to sell the same day.

**Downtime, spikes and snowballing**
- Downtime: day 1 (little to sell) and long showdown sequences in the late game.
- **Difficulty spike:** league stages 4 → 5 → 6 for an average player (38% → 11% → 5%).
- **Snowball:** the day-5 leader wins well above fair, and blowouts are common. The poorest-first buying rule does help:
  the old rotating order had 62% blowouts in 3-player games against 45% now. But being poor enough to buy first on 4 or
  more days still means winning only 15.5% of the time.
- **Unrecoverable:** a shop with less than $1 and no ripe fruit can't buy again without a lucky favour card.
- **Player count changes the game more than any setting:** 4-player games earn less than half what 2-player games earn.

## 6. Tuning and measurement plan

**Order of testing (expected impact first)**
1. **League steps (E1)**: no rules change, quick to simulate and ship.
2. **Player-count economy (E2)**: supply and demand coefficients, tuned **together** (their ratio matters), checked in 2, 3 and 4 players.
3. **Dead or weak choices (E4, E5, E6)**: Juice Press, Haggler, Big Basket, each **independently**, one change per test run.
4. **Snowballing (E3)** after the economy, because price wars and margins change it.
5. **Small fixes (E7, E8)**: tie-break and final-day events. Safe; ship with the next rules change.
6. **Difficulty and daily line-up (E11)** last, once the economy is settled.

**Tune together:** demand 0.40/0.45 with supply 1.0/0.5; customer top prices with fruit costs (margin); Signboard tip
with upgrade cost. **Tune alone:** each trait, each upgrade, each event, the league line-up.

**Telemetry to add** (anonymous counters, in line with `docs/ANALYTICS.md`; adding events changes data handling, so it
needs its own approved plan):
- Game result by mode, number of players, length and rival levels (win/lose, final rank, winning margin bucket).
- Trait picked × win; upgrades built (which, which day) × win.
- Fruit spoiled and unsold at the end (buckets); fruit sold; share of sales at the customer's top price.
- Rank on day 5 × final rank (snowball and comeback).
- Game duration in minutes (bucket) and minutes per day.
- Times a player was broke (cash < $1, no ripe stock).
- League: attempts per stage and clears; daily: rank against the three rivals.
