## 3. Checked and correct

- **League stage set-ups:** events, orders, customers, lorries, opening day, opening step and cash, checked over 2,562 engine games.
- **Old League progress:** no stage shows fewer stars.
- **Stage options:** ignored outside the League.
- **Saves:** reload and Continue work in every mode.
- **Takings words:** match the saved game on all 30 day-closes checked.
- **REP-133 fixes:** all confirmed.
- **Online:** 0 leaks. Old phones (computer-player version 7, protocol 4) are refused with the update message, and 170 games played identically on the old and new engines.
- **The Sprint's best-5 week:** works.
- **Screens:** no sideways scroll and no page errors on any screen.

- **RA-07 (balance; needs its own plan):** the expert stages are easier than their base stages. An Easy computer clears Expert 2 and stages 8 and 9 on their fixed seeds.
- **RA-14 (balance; needs its own plan):** in the Sprint, Ah Chen wins 42%. The cause is the Charmer perk's starting favour cards in a 3-day game. Giving Rosa that perk turns the result round.
- **Minor and Polish:**
  - RA-09: host lines after a shared win.
  - RA-10: days counted from day 1 on stage 10.
  - RA-12: an old 1.25 League game finishes under the new stage title.
  - RA-13: the Sprint is named as the Daily in an old-version notice.
  - B-05: the "Perk" label's contrast is 3.9:1.
  - P-01 to P-10: punctuation of the Play menu reasons; "Village Square" as both stage and chapter name; the Daily card's League line shows on most days; a loss's "decisive moment" can be a small sale; the weekly line says 7 days when 5 count; the old-version message mentions playing online; the reaction button sits below the fold at 320; keyboard focus on the League road; the next-trophy tie-break; small text in the Wardrobe and Stamp book; How to play does not mention the League or the Sprint.
