## 1. What changed for players

- **No forecast (rules 13).** Tomorrow's event is no longer shown the evening before. From day 2, the top event card is turned over each morning and applies that day. The Health Inspector is still never the last day's event (it is redrawn).
- **Walk-in events.** Tomorrow's walk-ins are dealt in the evening without knowing the event. Festival, Tourist Bus and Monsoon add or remove face-down walk-ins on the morning they are turned over. Walk-ins sent away by the Monsoon go back under the customer deck.
- **No Cold Room.** Three upgrades remain: Juice Bar $2, Handcart $3, Premium Display $4. Heatwave and Health Inspector no longer mention it. The "fully fitted" achievement now needs all three upgrades.
- **Morning card.** The day count, your standing and "tomorrow's news" are gone. The event picture fills the card, with a band underneath: "Today's news", the name and what it does. The card flips in when drawn.
- **Evening panel.** The warnings that depended on tomorrow's event (tired fruit and Inspector fines tomorrow) are gone.
- **Computer players.** They no longer read tomorrow's event. A shop that would have leaned to the Cold Room leans to the Handcart.

## 4. Risks

- Players lose a planning tool: they can no longer hold fruit for tomorrow's price event or sell off tired fruit before the Inspector. Days feel more like a surprise; the Inspector and Heatwave hit harder because you can't prepare.
- Removing the Cold Room makes the Juice Bar the only upgrade that helps with old fruit.
- Older apps and unfinished rules 12 games are refused, as with every rules change; the daily board key becomes r13 with new seeds.
