**Tester questions (locked)**
1. In one sentence: what is this game about?
2. Think about day 3. A customer didn't buy from you. Why do you think that happened?
3. When your fruit got old, what happened to it and its price?
4. What was the most confusing moment? Which screen or day was it?
5. What did you enjoy most? What annoyed you most?
6. Did you finish the game? If you stopped, on which day and why?
7. Would you play again? Why or why not?
8. Anything that looked broken or too small to read? (Phone model and browser, if known)

Testers play the preview from a browser link, one Quick game against one computer shop, without help.

**Rule for changes:** criteria and questions are not changed once results are read. A change is recorded before any result is read, with the reason.
