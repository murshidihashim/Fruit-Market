## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

**How I read the request.** "implement all the fix except for the price ladder" = build the tie-break and write the table procedures into the rules, without the ladder (fix 1) or the colour zones (fix 5, which sit on the ladder). A reader announcing which freshness qualifies at each price replaces the zones. If you wanted printed art, that is a separate step.

**In the code and the app**
- `src/engine/game.ts`: the poorer-shop step is gone from the tie-break; the order is now Favoured Trade, Haggler, fresher fruit, today's buying order.
- `src/engine/story.ts` and `src/ui/HowTo.tsx`: the explanations say "bought earlier today".
- `tests/rules4.test.ts`: the tie test now checks that the earlier buyer wins whatever the cash is.

**In the rules (`RULES.md`, the tabletop master):** the tie line, and a new "Playing on a table" section: customer order, the reader (announces the real top price and which freshness qualifies, and pays from the bank), order circles, the sole-supplier cube, the dawn line-up, evening for card holders only, day chips with age cards for blueberry, watermelon, apple and mango (with Cold Room, Fridge and Heatwave handled), and a waiting pile.

**Why the tie-break is safe.** Same 300 games with and without the change, computer players only: average final cash per shop 4 shops $146.9 to $146.3, 2 shops $145.9 to $145.9. Individual games differ because one different tie changes later choices; that does not show a balance change.
