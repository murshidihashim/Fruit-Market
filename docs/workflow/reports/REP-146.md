## 1. What changed from REP-145

- **Behind Rosa:** faint warm-beige bunting hangs under the tabs; faint crate outlines sit behind her bubble.
- **Rosa is lower:** her shoes stand on the card's striped awning; her bubble moved down with her.
- **Custom match:** four bordered tiles, each with a flat icon: Quick Match (bolt, blue), Private Room (key, red), Computer (monitor, green), Pass & Play (swap arrows, yellow).
- **Selected tile:** pale yellow fill, a heavier border and a tick in its corner.
- **Play names the choice** on the Custom card ("Play · Computer"). The other modes say "Play".

## 2. A change to where the buttons lead

- Today: a tile on Home's Custom card opens its page at once, and Play opens a Play page that lists the same four ways.
- Proposed: a tile only chooses. Play opens the chosen way's own page (the search, your room, or set-up).
- So the Play page is no longer reached from Home. Whether it stays for other entrances is for the plan.
- Each way is two taps (choose, then Play), or one when the choice is already ticked.
- The mockup starts on Computer. The game would remember the last choice.

## 3. Notes

- The icon colours are the game's own four fruit colours (#3558a2, #e73c35, #0e9a58, #fec400), the nearest to the ones listed, so the game keeps one palette.
- "Create room" was not used for Private Room: its page also joins a room by code, so the button says "Play · Private Room".
- Rosa's body below the shoulders is still a sketch drawn for the mockup.
- The mockup was looked at at 360 x 640 only. Other sizes: NOT RUN. Game tests: NOT APPLICABLE.
