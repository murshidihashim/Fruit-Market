## 3. Finding

**The price was not the problem.** Cheaper Cold Room and Juice Bar did not help the computers win with them.

- Supply is tight in rules version 4. The computers sell almost everything while it's fresh, and spoilage is near zero
  (REP-033). So the Juice Bar has little old fruit to work with, and holding stock in a Cold Room rarely pays.
- These two specialities reward plans the computers don't play: buying cheap old fruit on purpose, and holding stock
  for a Market Day.
- [Hyp] People may use them better. The cheaper prices make them easier to try in the playtest.
