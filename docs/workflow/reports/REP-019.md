## The idea in one line
**You sit at the market table.** Every game screen is the same board, like the current game, now with
the cleaner, pixel-art, warm look.

## The table
- **Street (top):** customers queue as pixel figures with a small tag ("apple ×2 · up to $10") and one
  plain hint ("3 shops sell apples").
- **Middle:** Foxy Rosa's stall, the Farmers market, Patches' stall, each under a striped awning.
  Rival stalls show only what matters: cash and what they hold.
- **Bottom:** your stall, with your fruit and how many nights each has left.

## Each phase on the same table
| Phase | What happens on the board |
|---|---|
| Buying | Tap crates in the market; fruit hops onto your stall. Foxy Rosa whispers. |
| Pricing | Tap a customer on the street to serve them. Rival stalls show sealed boards ("sealed…"). Your stall becomes your price board, which only you see. "Seal my prices". |
| Reveal | Ring the bell: the boards flip **on the stalls** (Patches $10, you $9, Foxy Rosa $8). Foxy Rosa's stall glows, coins fly to it, Grandpa Tan walks over, then "Outfoxed by $1!". |
| Evening | Night falls over the same table: lanterns on the rival stalls ("plotting…"), the market covered, and the upgrades at your stall. Favour card kept low, as in your earlier edit. |
