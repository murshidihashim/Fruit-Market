## 1. The game on one page

**Pitch.** You run a fruit stall in a busy harbour market. Each day you buy fruit from the farmers' lorry, guess what
the crowd will want and what your rivals will charge, then post your prices in secret. The cheapest stall makes the
sale. But fruit goes off every night, so stock you can't sell is money rotting on your shelf. After 8, 10 or 14 days,
the richest stall wins.

| | |
|---|---|
| Players | 2–4 (solo against computer shops in the app) |
| Length | Quick 8, Standard 10 or Long 14 days. App: about 15–20 minutes. Table: about 45–60 minutes **[Hyp]** |
| Age / weight | 10+. Light-to-medium strategy **[Hyp]**: simple turns, with depth in reading the market and rivals |
| Win condition | Most cash at the end. Unsold fruit and upgrades are worth nothing. Ties go to the shop that sold more fruit |
| Elimination | None. Everyone plays every day |
| Luck vs skill | Luck from the order of the farmer, customer and event decks, softened by a one-day look ahead. Skill in buying, timing, pricing and reading rivals. [Sim] Hard computer beats Normal 62% |

**The four pillars (your brief).**

| Pillar | How the game delivers it |
|---|---|
| **A real market** | Supply comes off a lorry; demand comes from customers with budgets. One posted price per fruit; the cheapest stall sells first. The market pulse moves prices up after a shortage and down after a glut |
| **Inventory and decay** | Every fruit ages one step a night and sells for less as it ages; the oldest spoils. Shelves are limited. Fruits rot at different speeds |
| **Demand forecasting** | The crowd queues a day ahead: the expected customer is face up, and the walk-ins are face down with their wanted fruits printed on the back. Tomorrow's event and tomorrow's lorry are also shown a day early |
| **Strategy and undercutting** | Sealed simultaneous pricing creates the "do I go \$1 lower?" duel. Eight different plans are viable (section 4) |

## 2. Components (tabletop)

| Component | Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Fruit tokens | 4 kinds (about 40 each) | Blueberry, watermelon, apple, mango |
| Market board | 1 | Farmers market (crates by arrival day), customer queue, market pulse track, day track |
| Shop mats | 4 | Shelf (8 spaces), crates by arrival day, "for sale" crate |
| Price boards and screens | 4 | One slider per fruit (\$1–\$14), hidden until the reveal |
| Farmer cards | 20 | Tomorrow's lorry: 3 fruits in quantity plus 1 of the fourth; some mark one fruit as arriving green |
| Customer cards | 24 | Front: name, wanted fruits, quantity, top price for fresh fruit. Back: fruit icons only |
| Event cards | 13 | One a day from Day 2, each announced a day early |
| Favour cards | 12 (6 kinds × 2) | One-off bonuses earned by serving a whole order alone |
| Upgrade cards | 6 | Evening draft |
| Trait cards | 6 | Pick 1 of 3 at the start |
| Coins, cubes, markers | | Cash; one market pulse cube per fruit; day marker |

**Crates instead of sliding.** Fruit sits in a crate marked with the day it arrived. Its age (today minus that day) gives
its freshness from the chart on the mat. Nothing needs moving each night, and the app shows the same thing as a decay
track.

## 3. A day, step by step

1. **Morning**
   - Reveal today's event (announced yesterday) and turn up tomorrow's.
   - **The lorry arrives:** put today's farmer card's fruit into the farmers market. Green fruit goes to the green crate.
   - Turn up **tomorrow's lorry** card.
2. **Buying**
   - **Poorest shop first** (ties go round the table).
   - Each shop buys up to **4 fruits** (5 with a Handcart), paying the cost for that freshness.
   - Shelf limit 8 (14 with a Bigger Shelf).
3. **The crowd**
   - Customers still waiting from yesterday stand at the front.
   - Today's expected customer and today's walk-ins are turned face up.
   - Draw **tomorrow's queue**: 1 expected customer face up, and the walk-ins face down (backs show their fruits).
4. **Pricing (secret)**
   - Behind your screen, set **one price per fruit** on your price board.
   - Put the fruit you are willing to sell in your "for sale" crate.
   - Everyone reveals at once.
5. **Sales**
   - Customers are served in queue order. For each fruit a customer wants, they buy from the **cheapest stall**.
   - Your price must not exceed what they pay for your fruit's freshness: their top price + market pulse + today's event −
     the age drop.
   - You hand over your oldest fruit that is fresh enough.
   - **Ties:** a Haggler facing a richer shop, then fresher fruit, then Favoured Trade, then turn order.
   - Paid on the spot.
   - Serve a customer's whole order by yourself to draw a **favour card** (one a day).
   - An unserved new customer waits until tomorrow; a waiting customer then leaves.
6. **Market pulse** (for each fruit)
   - **Shortage:** a customer still wants it, so the cube goes up \$1 (to +\$2 at most).
   - **Glut:** at least players + 1 ripe ones are left at the farmers market, so the cube goes down \$1 (to −\$1 at least).
   - Otherwise the cube moves one step back towards 0.
7. **Evening**
   - Upgrade draft: each shop sees 3 upgrades and may build any it can afford.
   - Evening favour cards can be played.
8. **Night**
   - All fruit ages one step, and fruit past its last step spoils.
   - The Juice Press turns up to 3 spoiling fruits into \$2 each.
   - The Cool Room ages your fruit only every other night.
   - Night events apply (Health Inspector fines, Heatwave ageing).

**Supply and demand on a table (no formulas).**
- **Lorry:** the card's three fruits arrive at the printed number (2–4); the fourth fruit arrives as 1.
  - 2 players: one less of each listed fruit (minimum 1).
  - 4 players: one more of every fruit.
- **Walk-ins:** players − 2 in the first half of the game, players − 1 in the second half.
  - 2 players: 0 → 1.
  - 3 players: 1 → 2.
  - 4 players: 2 → 3.
- [Sim] These printed rules reproduce the old formula flow almost exactly. In 3 players, about 10 fruits a day are
  delivered, and customers want about 9 units a day early and 13 late.

**Optional rule: Market Days** (off by default, for the paper playtest).
- Every third day is a fair: +1 walk-in, and **no lorry**.
- Tomorrow's lorry shows "Market Day", so shops must stock up the day before.
- See section 6 for why it is optional.

## 4. Fruit: price, decay and playstyle

| | 🫐 Blueberry | 🍉 Watermelon | 🍎 Apple | 🥭 Mango |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Playstyle** | Quick flipper | Steady bulk seller | The keeper | Speculator |
| Lorry cost fresh / okay / not so / green | \$2 / \$1 / \$1 / – | \$3 / \$2 / \$1 / \$1 | \$3 / \$2 / \$1 / – | \$4 / \$2 / \$1 / \$3 |
| Customer top price (fresh) | \$4–7 | \$6–9 | \$5–9 | \$7–11 |
| Price drop by age, oldest → freshest | −4 · −2 · 0 | −3 · −1 · −1 · 0 · 0 | −2 · −1 · −1 · −1 · 0 · 0 · 0 | −3 · −1 · 0 · 0 |
| Shelf life | 3 nights | 5 nights (+1 green) | 7 nights | 4 nights (+2 green) |
| Risk | High: steepest decay | Medium | Lowest | Highest: wait to ripen, fast spoil, few delivered |
| [Sim] Net profit per fruit bought | \$3.51 | \$3.15 | \$3.00 | \$5.15 |
| Best moment | Sell the day you buy | Big orders, Heatwave | Stock for a Lorry Breakdown or shortage | Buy green before a Mango Craze or a shortage |

The more a fruit can hurt you, the more it pays.

## 5. Events, traits, upgrades and favours

**Events** (13 cards; one a day from Day 2, each shown a day early):

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Festival | Customers pay +\$3, plus an extra walk-in |
| Blueberry Glut *(new, replaces Slow Season)* | +1 blueberry per player delivered; blueberry customers pay −\$1 |
| Heatwave | Watermelons +\$4; all fruit ages an extra step tonight |
| Apple Blight *(new, replaces Rainy Day)* | No apples delivered; apple customers pay +\$1 |
| Mango Craze | Mangoes +\$3; the Influencer wants 3 |
| Superfood Trend | Blueberries +\$3; the Gym Club wants 4 |
| Good Harvest *(scaled)* | +1 of every fruit per 2 players (rounded up); lorry prices −\$1 |
| Bad Harvest | Half delivery (rounded up); customers pay +\$2 |
| Price Hike | Lorry prices +\$1 |
| Minimum Price | Price floors per fruit |
| Health Inspector | \$2 fine per not-so fruit tonight. Never the final day's event (draw again) |
| Lorry Breakdown | No delivery today |
| Hotel Order | The Hotel Chef orders 3 apples, 2 watermelons and 2 blueberries |

**Traits** (pick 1 of 3 at the start, public):

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Haggler | Wins ties against richer shops, even over fresher fruit |
| Big Basket | +1 fruit a day |
| Bargain Hunter | First fruit each day \$1 cheaper |
| Charmer | Start with 3 favour cards; hold 4 |
| Local Favourite | +\$4 per customer you serve completely alone |
| Nest Egg | Start with \$15 instead of \$10 |

[Sim] 31–38% wins each in 3 players.

**Upgrades** (evening draft, 3 offered, built once):

| Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Handcart | \$6 | Buy 5 a day |
| Signboard | \$8 | +\$1 per fruit sold |
| Cool Room | \$7 | Ages every other night; Heatwave-proof |
| Bigger Shelf | \$3 | 14 spaces |
| Juice Press | \$4 | +\$1 per older fruit sold, and up to 3 spoiling fruits a night become \$2 each |
| Shop Bell | \$4 | Draw 2 favour cards; hold 4 |

**Favour cards** (2 of each): Free Cash (\$3), Free Fruit, Good Riddance (sell ripe stock back at \$1 each), Fridge (all
fruit one step fresher), Favoured Trade (win ties today), Priority (buy first tomorrow).

## 6. Balance evidence (computer players)

| Measure | Rules 3 (app now) | **Rules 4 (this design)** |
|---|---|---|
| Win share of 8 plans, 3 players (fair 33%) | 23–37% | 25–42% (Ripen at the top) |
| Win share, 4 players (fair 25%) | 20–28% | 18–25% |
| Hard beats Normal (2 players) | 60% | 56–62% |
| Comeback from last at half-time (3p / 4p) | 26% / 19% | 28–32% / 18–19% |
| Price-war sales at \$2 or less (3p) | 6% | 5% |
| Quick (8 days): 4-plan table | – | 19 / 30 / 30 / 21 (even) |
| Long (14 days): 4-plan table | Reserve 42% | Ripen 49% (watch item) |
| Traits (3 players) | 30–37% | 31–38% |

**Market Days** (optional rule):
- Every third day with +1 walk-in gives the most even plans of anything tested (24–35%).
- It also gives 38% comebacks and spoilage halves, because shops stock ahead.
- But Hard and Normal computers become even (50%), because the computers don't plan ahead.
- [Hyp] People who plan ahead may make it the most skilful mode. The paper playtest decides.

**Open items for the build:**
- Shop Bell, Juice Press and Cool Room are worth little on their own under posted prices (12.8–14.5%, against 13.5% for no
  upgrade). They need retuning.
- Long games favour the ripening plan.
- The computer players need proper posted-price logic.

## 7. For your benchmark: mechanism profile

Mechanism names follow the usual board-game catalogue terms, so you can compare like for like.

| Mechanism | In Fruit Market | Weight in this game |
|---|---|---|
| Simultaneous action selection / sealed pricing | Secret price board, revealed together | Core |
| Lowest-price-wins competition (a reverse auction) | Cheapest stall serves the customer | Core |
| Dynamic market pricing | Market pulse after shortages and gluts; lorry prices by freshness | Core |
| Perishable inventory / resource decay | Fruit ages nightly, loses value, spoils | Core |
| Demand forecasting / hidden information | Face-down queue with fruit-icon backs; tomorrow's lorry and event shown | Core |
| Open market buying | Shared farmers market, first come, first served | Medium |
| Catch-up turn order | Poorest buys first | Medium |
| Variable player powers | Traits | Light |
| Card drafting | Evening upgrade draft (3 offered) | Light |
| Event deck with look ahead | One event a day, announced a day early | Medium |
| Contract fulfilment | Serve a whole order alone for a favour | Light |
| Engine building | Upgrades that pay each day | Light |

**Starting points for comparison** (well-known games sharing a mechanism; please verify details in your own benchmark):

| Game | Shared idea | Where Fruit Market differs |
|---|---|---|
| Power Grid | A resource market whose prices rise as supply runs out; the trailing player buys first | Fruit also rots, and selling prices are set by the players in secret |
| Food Chain Magnate | Customers buy from the cheapest restaurant; price-setting competition | Much lighter; demand is forecast from a visible queue rather than built by marketing |
| For Sale / Modern Art | Blind or sealed bidding duels | Bidding is for customers each day, and goods spoil |
| Monopoly (traditional) | Win by cash; rents and luck | No elimination, no property; every day is a fresh price duel; catch-up turn order |
| Catan (traditional) | Variable supply each turn | Supply is shown a day ahead and bought, not rolled; no trading between players |
| Sushi Go / 7 Wonders | Simultaneous play and drafting | Drafting is a side system (upgrades); the core is pricing and stock |

**Possible distinctive hooks** for your benchmark to confirm: perishable stock with a different decay speed per good;
one posted price per good settled by a simultaneous reveal; and a self-adjusting price track driven by unmet demand.
