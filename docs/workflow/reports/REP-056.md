## SUMMARY

Four looks for the same board (same tracks, slots, prices and pixel fruit), each with the kit it would give the rest of the game (buttons, cash chip, customer order, event card, portrait): A stall front, B chalkboard, C crate rack, D soft tabletop mat. File: Board_Look_Options.html.

## RECOMMENDATION

D: calmest, lightest to print, cheapest to build and easiest to read on a small screen. Its weak point is less market character, which the home scene, street and lorry can carry. A is the safe familiar choice, C the most charming and most work, B the best fit with the dark screen UI.
