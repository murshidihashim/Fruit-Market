## 3. Findings

### Major

| ID | Where | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | One phone, 2 people | For about 0.6 seconds at the start of each buying turn, the next person's market and buttons work before "Pass the phone" appears. ## 4. Balance (for the owner; not part of the fix plan)

- **A-09:** In the Daily market, Uncle Benny (Hard) wins 57 to 66 percent of games, and your seat (played by a Normal computer) wins 14 to 16 percent. In the Sprint he wins 37 percent.
- **Rosa against Ah Chen:** the gap comes from Ah Chen's perk, not her seat. Her 3 starting favour cards are worth about $4.70 of extra takings in a 3-day Sprint. Over 10 days the gap is small.
- **Changing either is a balance change** that needs its own plan. For example, Benny could play the Daily and Sprint at Normal.
