## 2. Journey, animation and sound

- **Home:** four labelled buttons under the mode cards: Play, League, Daily, Sprint. Each waiting game shows its day.
  - Continue names the mode, day and cash.
  - Browser Back goes Home and keeps the save.
  - The fullscreen question waits until the player is back Home after a game.
  - Home still fits 390x844 and 360x640.
- **Play:** the stocking button now reads "Set secret prices".
- **Freshness words:** one set (Green, Fresh, Okay, Soft) on the market, shelf, crate, cards and Rush.
- **Tags:** "Spoils tonight" and "Loses Premium tomorrow" on the stocking rows.
- **Customer cards:** the spoken words match their lamps for Premium, Everyday and Bargain.
- **Bell:** every crate opens and every price turns over together, in 2.0 s at any table size, and stays readable.
- **Rush:** a "+$N" lands at your cash.
- **Results:**
  - "Places at this table" is kept apart from the Daily or Sprint board.
  - One deciding moment, taken from the game's record.
  - A plain recap when you made no sales.
  - The Sprint box comes first, with your best and your perk.
  - League stars are tagged New, Met again, Earned before or Missed, and the next step is written out.
- **Settings:**
  - New "Reduced motion" switch. It also follows the phone's setting.
  - "Fast rivals" is now "Fast play".
  - Mute and Reduced motion survive a reload.
- **Found in this check and fixed:** at 360 px the longer Rush count ran under the customers' faces. It now wraps.
