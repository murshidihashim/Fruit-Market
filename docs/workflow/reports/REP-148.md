## 1. What was built

The mockup of REP-147, less the "Next goal" line, is now the game's Home (Market tab):

- Top to bottom: fruit band, title with the profile chip and the gear, the mode tabs (Match, League, Daily, Sprint), Rosa and her bubble, one wide card, Play.
- Rosa: full body, the same in every mode. She walks in, sways, blinks, tilts her head, waves her price tag and taps her foot; she hops and talks for each new line. A tap on her or her bubble gives another line.
- Cards: the mode's name with where you stand beside it ("28 Nov · Not played", "Stage 1 of 12", "Day 4 of 10 · $23").
  - Match: four tiles with pictures. A tile chooses; the chosen one is ticked and remembered; Play says "Play · Computer" and opens that way's page.
  - League: the stage's picture, title, goal, stars and the next trophy.
  - Daily and Sprint: the first customer (a tap opens the whole card), the lorry in four large tiles, one line of rules.
- A sideways swipe on the card changes mode, as the tabs do.
- "Custom match" is "Match" everywhere.

## 2. Where the game differs from the mockup

- The next card does not peek in at the edge. A swipe slides the new card in.
- Rosa's line for the Daily and the Sprint names the day's real first customer plainly ("Coral Mei is first in line today."). On a day that opens with an event she says the market is ready.
- Her League line names the stage and its rival; "That stall is mine!" only when the rival is Rosa.
- "Start over" on a waiting Match still opens the Play page with the four ways.
- A first customer's picture still opens the whole customer card on a tap, as before.
- Continue is one word; a screen reader still hears which game it continues.
