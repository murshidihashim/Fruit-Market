## 1. What the owner's words were taken to mean

"Build part 2 as well" approves part 1 (PLAN-132) and part 2 (the player number). The three open questions of REP-147 were not answered, so the recommended answer was used for each, and each is easy to change:

1. Records are kept **12 months**.
2. The player number is **asked for once** ("Help improve the game?"), not on by default.
3. Everything stays on **the game's own server**; no third-party service.

## 2. What was built

**The record (`src/net/matchRecord.ts`).** A game's set-up, a sheet for each day (every stall's purchases, crate and prices, every sale line, cash, spoiled fruit, upgrades, standing-order bids), and how it ended. Games on one phone add each move with the time it took, the take-backs, the cards and rule pages opened, a coarse device note and a count of long pauses.

**A change from the plan.** The plan said a record would be the seed and the moves, replayed later. Online matches cannot be replayed that simply (a computer can take over a seat, purchases can be taken back), and a replay breaks whenever the computer players change. So a record stores each day's facts directly, for every mode alike, and nothing has to be replayed to read it. The moves and timings are still kept for games on one phone.

**The gate.** The server rebuilds every record field by field (`cleanRecord`): numbers are range-checked and every string must be one of the game's own words (fruit, events, customers, perks, upgrades) or random hex. A name cannot pass it, whatever an app sends.

**On the phone.** `src/net/recorder.ts` keeps the record beside the saved game and sends it at the end of each day, at the end, and when the game is left (with the step and the leaver's rank). With "Share game data" off, nothing is kept or sent.

**Online.** The room server keeps the record and stores it when the match ends, only if every person's "Share game data" is on. An older app says nothing and counts as off. The record is never sent to a player.

**The store.** A new `RecordsDO` on the game server, one per month, deleting itself after 12 months. `GET /api/matches` (key needed) downloads a month.

**Part 2, the player number (`src/net/player.ts`).** Asked once on Home after a first game. Yes makes 16 random hex digits on the phone; records then carry it with a session number, games this session, install day, test bucket, stall level, league stage and looks unlocked, and each app start sends one line. Settings has "Link my games" and "Forget my data", which deletes the number's records on the server and the number on the phone.

**New counts.** First buy, first sale, Day 1 done; each mode's page viewed; the step a game is left at.

**Reader.** `npm run matches` prints games by mode, where games are left, the price contest, wins by people, computer level and perk, time per move, take-backs, cards opened, devices, and for players with a number: games per player, return by first mode, levels, test buckets.

**Words.** The Settings switch is "Share game data"; the privacy page has new sections "Game data" and "A player number"; `docs/ANALYTICS.md` and the project's privacy rule in `CLAUDE.md` are updated.

## 4. Checks

See PLAN-132 and PLAN-133 for the results as recorded.

- A real run on a local copy of the game server: two Sprint games and two online matches played in a browser each produced one record (4.9 to 7.7 KB); a record with a name in it was refused; a session line and Forget my data worked; a download without the key was refused.

## 5. Size and cost

- Measured: a 3-day Sprint about 5 KB; a 10-day online match at 2 stalls 5.5 to 7.7 KB. A 10-day game at 4 stalls with its moves is estimated at 10 to 15 KB (not measured). That is larger than the 3 to 6 KB guessed in REP-146.
- At 15 KB, 1,000 games are about 15 MB. A month's store holds what that month receives.
- Cloudflare's published limit for this kind of storage on the free plan is 5 GB in total, as far as I know; that would be about 300,000 games. I could not read the account's plan, so this is not confirmed.
- Money: RM0 expected.
