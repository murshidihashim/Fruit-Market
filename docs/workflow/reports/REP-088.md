## 1. How to answer

Reply with three things, for example: *"Style A. Change 6, 13, 20, S3. Also make your Fix changes. Name 13 'Ken'."*

1. **Which names you dislike:** customers by number (1 to 36), shopkeepers S1 to S6, guests G1 to G3. Add a new name if you have one in mind; otherwise I will suggest some.
2. **Which style:** A (recommended), B or C (section 3).
3. **My fixes:** should I also make the changes marked **Fix** in section 4, even where you didn't pick the name (recommended)? Or change only the ones you choose?

## 2. The short version

1. Most names are fine. 21 of the 45 need no change, and 6 more keep their name and only get a new one-line joke (quirk).
2. **Three pairs sound alike** when the crier reads names aloud: Ben and Benny, Wei Jie and Jia Wen (the same two sounds), and Hafiz and Hadi. One name in each pair should change. The pilots, Benny and Jia Wen, stay.
3. **Four titles hint at a hidden card type:** Captain (Premium), Rockstar (Bargain), Dr (generous) and Farid's "Or pay" joke (Bargain). Look E's rules ban that (ART_DIRECTION §9).
4. **Some jokes are about who someone is, not what they do:** Sleepy Zul (an echo of the "lazy" stereotype), a Japanese name given judo, and two Chinese grandfathers who both grumble about prices.
5. **The culture spread is lopsided:** none of the six Chinese-named customers is Premium, and four of them read as thrifty. A rename can fix this without moving any card's type.
6. **Changing a name never changes the game.** Each card keeps its type, wants and prices, because the old name stays as a hidden ID in the code.

## 6. Rules every new name must follow

- A job or hobby a child can picture and an artist can draw as one prop. The joke is what they do, never who they are (DEC-018, DEC-039, DEC-041).
- No rank, fame, money, thrift or fussy-taste words, so no name hints at Premium, Bargain or generous.
- Sounds clearly different from every other name when read aloud.
- About 16 letters or fewer, so the card header stays large.
- No culture tied to a job, and no famous people, brands or well-known fictional characters.
- The pilots keep their approved looks: Benny (broad, bearded, Big Basket) and Sailor Jia Wen (navy, sailor's cap, rolled mango map).

## 7. What happens after you choose

1. I record your choices as a decision with your exact words, and update the creative bible's cast list.
2. The design package continues with the final names: Character Bible v1.0 for the pilots, reference sheets and prompts, card mock-ups and mobile specifications (PLAN-048).
3. **Later, in a separate implementation plan:** the names change in the game, the printed kit and RULES.md. Old names stay as hidden IDs, so saved games, online rooms and every card's type, wants and prices stay the same. RULES.md's list of generous customers is rebuilt from the game at the same time (today it lists 12, and the game has 13; REP-087 §11).

## 8. Evidence and limits

- **Sources:** names, titles, quirks, types and generous customers were checked against `src/engine/data.ts` and the game's generous rule. Sprite details come from `src/ui/pixel.tsx`.
- **Method:** one AI audit of all 45 names, two AI style proposals (Kickstarter appeal; clarity read aloud), and one AI reviewer who tried to break the proposals. Their fixes are in this version.
- **NOT RUN:** a read-aloud test with a real crier, and a header-fit test on a printed card. Both are needed before names are final.
