## 1. What changes for players

- **Home** is now your stall, drawn with every look you wear, followed by three swipeable cards: Play, Market League and Daily market (with the Sprint).
- **The Play page** keeps the normal ways to play; the League and the Daily moved to their own cards.
- **A new intro screen** explains the Daily market or the Sprint before a game starts: the goal, today's twist, the rivals, how you are ranked, and retries.
- **Each mode keeps its own unfinished game** on its card, so starting one mode never replaces another mode's game. A save from an older version moves to its mode by itself.
