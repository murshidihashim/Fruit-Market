## 1. Changes

**Option 2: more fruit (rule change).** Every fruit listed on a farmer card brings 1 more. Printed values are now 3–5
(were 2–4). The unlisted fruit still comes as 1; the 2-shop and 4-shop adjustments are unchanged. `RULES.md` updated.

**Option 1: smarter computers (no rule change).** Normal and Hard computers now:
- with a **Juice Bar**, count the \$1 tip on older fruit and the \$2 juice floor, so cheap old fruit is worth buying;
- with a **Cold Room**, value stock as lasting about twice as long, and hold back on the nights their fruit doesn't age;
- stock up harder the day before a **Market Day**;
- pick the speciality that suits them (old fruit about → Juice Bar; Market Days to come → Cold Room), so plans vary
  across the table.

Easy computers are unchanged.

## 3. What this means

**Good.**
- Skill now matters clearly: Hard beats Normal 60% (the earlier aim was 62–65%), and Easy is a real beginner level.
- Comebacks rose to 38%, and day-5 leaders win less often. Games stay open longer.
- Customers are served more often (46% of wanted fruit, was 31%).

**Not fixed: Cold Room and Juice Bar.**
- The REP-033 "rush" test was misleading. A shop that rushes one speciality also skips the Barrow, and skipping the
  Barrow alone drops a shop to 16%.
- The fairer measure is "skip it": skipping Cold Room or Juice Bar still makes no difference (33–34%). These two are
  harmless but add little. Even with the smarter computers, almost no fruit spoils, so the Juice Bar has little to do.

**New problem: volume wins.**
- With more fruit, buying more each day is the strongest plan: Big Basket wins 45% and the Barrow is close to
  essential.
- 2-player blowouts rose to 42%.
