## STATUS

A printable prototype exists: one A3 landscape mat and three A4 pages (two token sheets and a "what to test" page). A PDF render shows 4 pages at the right sizes (A3 landscape then A4 portrait). It has not been printed on paper. The screen market is not started.

## SUMMARY

The mat is the Look C board: flat, smooth frame, awning, sign and slots, with the pixel fruit inside. Four tracks, one per fruit (3, 6, 7 and 6 slots, newest at the top). Every slot has the printed price from the game's cost table, a freshness band, a stage word, a "nights left" label and a faint fruit; gold slots are where the lorry delivers. The footer has a lorry, spots for the farmer and event cards (lying down) and a legend. Fruit tokens are 20 mm, 30 of each fruit.

## EVIDENCE: HOW MANY TOKENS

Computer-player games (200 per row, events on, 10 days). Most of one fruit ever in play (market and all shelves): 2 shops blueberry 14, watermelon 21, apple 17, mango 18; 3 shops 20, 23, 20, 22; 4 shops 27, 25, 23, 23. One market slot held up to 14, so tokens stack. Thirty of each fruit covers the highest figure with a margin. People may hold more than computers do; the playtest will tell.

## THINGS TO DECIDE OR TEST

1. **Night handling:** the prototype slides each pile down a slot at night. The current table rules use a fruit clock and day chips instead (nothing moves). Test which is quicker with 4 shops. This changes the "Playing on a table" text if the board wins.
2. **Stage on tokens:** the token always shows the fresh picture and the slot gives the stage; on the screen the picture dulls. Decide if table tokens should change.
3. **Shelves:** each shop's own stock also needs a track (smaller). Not on this board yet.
4. **Print:** A3 at 100%, background graphics on; slot 22 mm; the apple column (7 slots) fits with a 7 mm margin.
5. **Fonts:** the file loads Pixelify Sans and Fredoka online; print with internet on.
