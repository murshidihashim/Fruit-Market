## 2. Removing Closing Day: what changes for players

- **The last day is an ordinary day.** Customers pay the usual prices, with no extra $1.
- **There is no jam maker.** Fruit left at the end is worth nothing, so the last day is about selling everything you can.
- **Trophy "Nothing for the jam maker".**
  - The condition stays the same: sell 20 or more pieces and end with no ripe fruit.
  - It gets a new name. My pick is "Sold out".
  - Stamps already earned are kept.
- **This is a rules change: rules version 19 becomes 20.** As with every rules change:
  - a game saved halfway on 1.24.0 cannot be continued (the app says so and starts fresh);
  - phones on 1.24.0 are asked to update before joining a room;
  - the Daily leaderboard starts a new board.
- **Everywhere the rules are written, Closing Day goes:** RULES.md, How to play, the tips, the guidebook, the player aid and the counter card.
- **Balance:**
  - The game's computer-player test runs before and after the change.
  - If any shopkeeper's share of wins moves by more than 3 points, you see it before anything is published.
  - C-01 changes the Daily and League perk deal in the same rules version.
