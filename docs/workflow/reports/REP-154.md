## 2. The player number: what it buys and what it costs

**What it is.** A random number made on the phone at first start. It is not a name, account, phone ID or advertising ID. It is sent with each record and count.

**What it buys.** Everything that follows one player over time: day 1, 7 and 30 return by what they played first; sessions; how fast levels come; whether better players win more; A/B tests; and later, ratings.

**What it costs.**
- The app's own promise changes. Settings says today: "No names or IDs are sent." The privacy page says the install date stays on the phone. Both would be rewritten.
- It is no longer anonymous data in the strict sense, but pseudonymous: one person's games can be tied together, though not to who they are. Privacy laws treat that as personal data. The audience includes players from 12 years old, and the Android release goes through Google Play's data safety form, which would have to declare it.
- Players need a way to reset the number (a "Forget my data" button), and the server a way to delete by number.

**Recommendation.** Do it, but as part 2, with a one-time question on first start ("Help improve Fruit Market by sharing game data? No name is ever sent") rather than on by default, a reset button, and a rewritten privacy page. I am not a lawyer; before the Play release the privacy wording deserves a check by someone who is.

## 3. Why not a third-party analytics service yet

- The game server already takes counts (`StatsDO`) and would hold the records; nothing new to pay for or set up.
- A third party receives players' data, needs its own entry in the privacy page and the Play form, adds a dependency, and usually sets its own device ID.
- If the built-in tables become too limiting, PostHog or a Supabase table can be added later from the same events. The work in part 1 is not wasted.

## 4. The plan in two parts

**Part 1: no ID (PLAN-133).** Match records with timing, undo counts and tooltip opens; the first-game and menu funnels as counts; quit context; coarse device and slow-frame buckets; daily saving of the record. Still no way to link two games.

**Part 2: the player number (a new plan after part 1 ships).** The random number with a first-start question and a reset button; sessions; return by first mode; progression speed; A/B tags.
