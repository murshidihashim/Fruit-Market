## 1. How it was done

Three reviewers each took one area. Each built their own copy of 1.24.0 on this machine and played it there. The live
site was not used, and no Quick match or Daily market was played on it.

- **Solo and pass-and-play:**
  - 9 whole games: 2, 4 and 6 stalls; 8, 10 and 14 days; 3 phone sizes; reduced motion on and off; one 3-person pass-and-play game.
  - Set scenarios for every favour card, ties, Priority, standing orders, the Health Inspector, a full shelf, reloading at every step, Rematch and Share, and all menus.
  - Some scenarios needed a hand-made game state, and the review says which ones.
- **Online:** a local room server (the same code as the live one) with up to three browsers. It covered:
  - private rooms, quick match, choosing shopkeepers, the first-time popup and the FIRST token;
  - leaving and coming back, computer takeover and rematch;
  - an older version of the app;
  - secret prices (624 room messages checked).
- **Consistency:** RULES.md, the app's words, the print kit, the guidebook and the update pack, read against the game's
  own numbers (`src/engine/data.ts`), plus the new colours and badges.

How sure: **seen** means it happened on screen and has a picture. **Likely** means it was read in the code and agrees
with what was seen. **Possible** means it was not confirmed. The Major findings were also checked against the code by me
before this report (S1, S2, ON-01, ON-03, ON-06) and against the pictures (S1, S2, S3).

## 2. The result in one paragraph

No crash, no stuck game, no wrong score at the end, and no secret leaked online (0 in 624 messages). The rules engine
agrees with RULES.md, and no number in RULES.md, the guidebook, the cards or the app disagrees with the game's data.
There are **56 findings, none blocking: 9 Major, 25 Minor, 22 Polish.** The Major ones are three wrong or misleading
screens in a normal game and six online situations where a player is stuck with no way out. Several Minor ones are on
the **printed cards, player aid and guidebook**, so the print is best held until they are fixed.

## 3. Major (9) · recommended: fix now, one package

| ID | What happens | Sure | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Wrong places on the last day.** The final takings list leaves out the rivals' jam-maker money, so the places differ from the results one tap later. In one game it showed you 5th, then the results showed you 6th. | seen, 5 games | Work out everyone's jam money for that list from the full game, as the results do |
| S2 | **Delivery day can cost you the stake.** On the day a standing order is collected, "N wanted" and Auto load ignore the order. In the worst case the screen said "Nobody wants these" and "Sell nothing today"; following it delivered 0 of 3 and lost the $6 stake. Normally Auto load just loads too few. | seen (worst case in a hand-made state) | Count the order's pieces as wanted on its day, load them, and say "3 for The Beach Resort" |
| S3 | **Scout can be used again and again.** Play Scout, look at a walk-in, tap Undo: the card comes back and the walk-in turns face down. You can see every walk-in for free. Undo also gives back Fridge and Free Fruit, though the button says "Undo purchases". | seen | Undo takes back purchases only; a favour card once played stays played (recommended), or no Undo after Scout |
| ON-01 | **Quick matches are played with upgrades**, but the rules (rules 8, REP-086) say quick matches have none. The settings are reset when the second stranger arrives. | seen | Keep quick matches without upgrades, as the rules say (recommended). Or, if you now want upgrades there, change the rules text instead |
| ON-02 | **"Create a room" breaks after an invite link.** It keeps showing the old invite (or drops you back into the old room) until the app is reloaded. | seen | Forget the invite once it has been used |
| ON-03 | **A wrong or expired room code never says so.** "Connecting to room ZZZZZZ…" stays for ever. | seen (wrong code), likely (expired) | Answer "That room doesn't exist or has closed", with a way back |
| ON-04 | **Rematch can wait for ever** when one person has gone Home: "Waiting for Benny B to accept…". | seen | Count only the people still there, and say "Benny B has left" |
| ON-05 | **One closed browser tab blocks a private lobby.** All seats show READY, but the host's button waits for ever and the seat cannot be removed. | seen | Free a disconnected lobby seat after the usual wait (quick lobbies already do this), and say who is disconnected |
| ON-06 | **Android app only: invite links likely point nowhere** (`https://localhost/?room=…`), because the link is built from the app's own address. | likely (no Android phone here) | Build the link from the game's real address; check on a phone |

None of these changes the rules. ON-01 makes the app follow the rules already written. ON-03 to ON-05 change how the
room server handles people who are gone, which the online tests cover; their tests would grow with the fix.

## 6. Your design choices (6) · these touch balance, hidden information or the look

| ID | The clash | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| C-01 | In the Daily market and the League you can be dealt a rival's perk (45 of 51 Daily days checked). With two Nest Eggs, only one works: Saleh's printed perk silently does nothing | Deal only perks no rival holds. This changes the Daily's deal, so it needs a plan and a balance check first |
