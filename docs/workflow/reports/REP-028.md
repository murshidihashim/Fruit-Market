## 3. What did not work, and what I did instead

- **Long games (O4).** I tried 10 different levers in the lab:
  - demand growth, demand start, supply start, supply growth;
  - when the second walk-in arrives;
  - the Cool Room price;
  - and others.

  None stopped patient plans (Reserve and Ripen) winning most 14-day games; Reserve stays at 39–52%. Long mode
  therefore keeps the standard rules. [Hyp] Patience winning a long game may feel right to players. The playtest
  should check it.
- **Cool Room (O5).**
  - A cheaper Cool Room (\$4–5) adds little.
  - A Cool Room that stops ageing completely makes the Reserve plan too strong (45–49%).
  - Under the new market the current Cool Room is already worth buying (15.8%, against 10.8% for nothing), so it stays
    unchanged.
- **Juice Press (O5).** Four juice designs were tested. Juicing any leftover fruit was worse, because it turns good fruit
  into juice. The +\$1 "juice tip" on older fruit was the only design that helped without boosting any plan.
- **Skill gap (S3).** Hard beats Normal 60% of the time (target 65%). Most of the drop comes from green mango at \$3:
  without it the figure is about 70%, but Ripen and Turnover weaken.
  - Mango is on the preview as approved.
  - Changing it back to \$2 is a one-line change. Decide after playtesting.
  - [Hyp] A smaller gap makes the game friendlier to new players and less punishing against Hard.
