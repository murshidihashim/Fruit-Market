## SUMMARY

1. **Widths and layout.** A new sweep (scripts/qa-sweep.cjs) opened every screen at 7 sizes (small phone, phone, phone landscape, tablet both ways, laptop, desktop). No sideways overflow and no script errors anywhere. Screenshots looked right; on tablet and desktop the game is a centred column.
2. **Accessibility.** The sweep found 136 distinct text-contrast problems. Worst: the whole Trophies screen and the favour and upgrade chips in How to play were nearly invisible. Fixed. Now 3 left: "10 days" label, the SOLD stamp (fixed in the last commit) and a floating +$ number that is on screen for a moment. Also darker muted text, a slightly darker terracotta (so cream text on it passes), a darker teal on tips and the home buttons, and name labels on the set-up fields. Buttons that are 32 to 36 px tall (chips and market cells) were left as they are; they pass the 24 px minimum but not the 44 px ideal. Making them taller changes the board layout, which is your call.
3. **Online lobby.** Covered by the existing browser tests in CI (online match with a leaver, quick match, four players in one room, rejoin from a background tab). They passed. I did not add a new live-server test.
4. **Soak.** 1500 computer-only games over 2 to 4 players, 8, 10 and 14 days and nine option sets: none stuck, none crashed, no impossible numbers, same seed repeats the same game. Average length 10.6 days. Seat 2 wins most often (765 of 1500 against 399, 165, 171); that is the balance study's finding about computer levels, not a bug, and I changed nothing.
5. **Card backs.** Delivery, Favour and Event backs: Print_Kit/Card_Backs.pdf (draft design for your approval). Customer backs were already in the customer card PDF. The README is updated.
6. **Cleanup.** 19 unused style rules and one animation from the old look removed.
7. **Rulebook and tutorial.** RULES.md has a new "The big idea" section and a worked example (Hafiz buys 5 blueberries from three stalls), and two small typos fixed. How to play now opens with "The big idea" and "One customer, start to finish". This is new public wording: please read it.
