## STATUS

600 computer games per row (normal level, 10 days, upgrades and events on). Nothing in the game was changed.

## GOOD RIDDANCE

It is played rarely: 0.21 times a game with 2 players, 0.13 with 3, 0.10 with 4. A player who plays it wins less often than average (2 players 23% against 53%; 4 players 13% against 25%). That is mostly because the computer plays it when it is already stuck with old fruit, so it is a rescue card, not a winning move. Removing it, keeping one copy, or swapping it for Free Fruit or Fridge changes average cash by less than $1.5 and the winning margin by about 1 to 2 dollars: inside the noise. Seat order stays fair in all versions.

**What this means:** balance does not decide it. The question is feel: a card whose best use is dumping stock at $1 each. Scout (look at the front of one walk-in arriving today) cannot be simulated honestly because the computer does not use hidden information, so it needs a human playtest.

This is a rule change if acted on, so it is your decision: for example, change Big Basket to "buy 1 extra fruit on days 1 to 5", or make Haggler stronger.

- Leader after day 5 wins about the fair share: comebacks are common.
