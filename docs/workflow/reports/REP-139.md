## 1. What changes for players

- **Polish pass (PLAN-123, REP-138):**
  - The market never looks sold out while a rival buys, and the counters change together.
  - Stocking counts each fruit once.
  - The Rush words are clearer, and Skip to the end goes straight to the recap.
  - The Sprint and League words are clearer.
  - Freshness words are the same everywhere.
  - Results show places at this table and the deciding moment.
  - New Reduced motion setting.
- **New Home (PLAN-124):**
  - Market, Stall and Rules tabs at the bottom.
  - On Market, pick Custom, League, Daily or Sprint and press Play, or Continue a saved game.
  - On tablets and computers the column is wider. On phones the Custom card's four ways fill the card.
- **Four modes (PLAN-125):**
  - Quick Match (2, 4 or 6 stalls, with computers filling after 30 seconds), Private Room, Computer and Pass & Play.
  - A one-page set-up in the card style.
  - In Private Room, everyone chooses a shopkeeper after the host starts.
- **PLAN-122:** its centring and the Level box were replaced by PLAN-124's Home. Nothing of it is visible on its own any more.
