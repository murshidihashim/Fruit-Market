## STATUS

Art direction decided (DEC-033). Nothing built. Three plans wait for approval.

## SUMMARY

The art audit (Art_Style_Audit) found the game mixes pixel sprites, flat vector, CSS shapes and one smoothed raster lorry with no shared rule. The owner compared pixel everywhere (A), flat vector everywhere (B) and calm flat scenery with pixel objects (C), and chose C. The Farmers Market board will be drawn once and used for the screen and the printed table mat; league stages can later be skins of it. A concept prototype of an animated market opening exists.

## PLANS, IN ORDER

1. PLAN-027 style guide and one palette (small).
2. PLAN-028 the shared board, Village Square skin only (medium; touches the market, animation anchors and the printed set).
3. PLAN-029 animated opening (small to medium; shares sprites with PLAN-028).

## DECISIONS STILL OPEN

The lorry (redraw as flat scenery or keep the picture), the printed board size (A3 landscape is the working assumption; needs a print), the table token size, and whether to do the other league skins later.
