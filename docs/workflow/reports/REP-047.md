## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

**Supply (PLAN-020).** Evidence from code, not from play:
- No lorry comes on a Market Day (days 3, 6, 9, 12; `game.ts`, `RULES.md`). In a 10-day game that is 3 days with no new fruit.
- With 4 shops, every fruit gets +1 (`printedSupply`). A card brings about 17 fruits (three listed fruits at 3 to 5 plus 1 each, and the fourth fruit at 1, all +1). Four shops can each buy up to 4 fruits a day, so 16.
- Fruit also spoils overnight, so leftovers do not carry the market through a no-lorry day.
- This fits the complaint but is a hypothesis. The first step is to measure it by simulation: per day and per fruit, how much is on the market when each shop buys.
- Options for step 2, to choose after measuring: a smaller delivery on Market Days, or more supply for 4 shops. Either must work by hand with cards (DEC-027), so a simple printed number is preferred over a new card rule.
- Needs your target first: the largest share of shop turns that may find nothing to buy.

**Session length (PLAN-021).**
- The new-game screen defaults to 10 days (Standard); Quick is 8 (`App.tsx`). The 30-minute game used 10 rounds with 4 players.
- First steps with no rule change: default to Quick for 3 or 4 shops, and make waiting skippable or faster (lorry, reveal, computer turns), without hiding information.
- Time a Quick 4-shop game with a stopwatch before and after. Proposed target: 20 minutes or less (yours to set).
- Shortening the number of days is a rules change and stays out of this plan.

**UI requests, client only (PLAN-022).**
- Same icon: the default avatar is the first one unless a player picks another in the online lobby (`Online.tsx`). Proposed: each seat gets the first unused icon.
- Name: it falls back to "You" (`store.ts`). Proposed: blank field, focused, keyboard opens, with a fallback if left empty.

**Undo and rename in the room (PLAN-023).**
- Tapping a fruit sends the buy at once (`useGameController.ts`). Undo is a new legal action; online it must be checked by the server, tested for duplicates, stale actions and reconnect, and must not leak hidden information.
- Renaming inside a room needs a server message. Step 1 is a design note for your approval; no code until then.
