## 1. What changed from REP-143

- **Rosa is full body.** She stands on the left with no counter and no frame: white top, yellow apron, red skirt, a soft circle of her own colour behind her, two pieces of fruit at her feet.
- **She moves.** She walks in, bounces, blinks, tilts her head, waves her price tag and taps her foot. On a swipe she hops.
- **She explains the mode on the card showing.** Her bubble changes with the card; a tap on her gives a second line. She opens with a welcome.

- On 360 x 640 the cards are 166 px wide and carry less text than today.

- The mockup was looked at at 360 x 640 only. Other sizes: NOT RUN. Game tests: NOT APPLICABLE.
