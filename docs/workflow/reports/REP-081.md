## SUMMARY

- **Why pixel art:** the owner pointed out the first mockups did not match the characters. The art guide was unclear about the lorry; it now says props are pixel art (DEC-052).
- **Lorry:** the Sunny Pickup with the wooden bunting roof, 54 x 31 art pixels shown at 3x (162 px wide, up from 150). Wheels turn and the flags flutter in two frames while it arrives and leaves.
- **Stalls:** the stall panels (rivals, yours, and a rival's opened stall) get a tiled roof band in the player's colour (so unlocked colours still work). The market opening shows the tiled stall with its sign and each shopkeeper's portrait.
- **Release:** this is part of the 1.2.0 candidate.
