## 2. What was built

Everything in spec sections 1 to 9:

- **Core:** 2-6 stalls; $10 start; buy 4 (Handcart 5); shelf 6 (Handcart 8); orders halved (round half up, at least 1); apples $1 higher.
- **Crowd:** walk-ins = stalls − 1; Market Day and walk-in events scale per 3 stalls (table in RULES.md).
- **Lorries:** 20 different cards with rows for 2-6 stalls; the Seconds Lorry fills each fruit's freshest Okay space.
- **Events:** 20 cards. New: Tourist Bus, Monsoon Downpour, Durian Season, Kenduri (Wedding Caterer), Mango Windfall, Pasar Malam (night hawker), Bake-Off (Bakery). Glut and Good Harvest scale per 2 and per 4 stalls.
- **Shopkeepers:** Pak Din (Shade Cloth: a person picks the fruit in the evening; the suggested fruit is used if they don't) and Farah (Sharp Ears: peeks at the first unseen walk-in in tomorrow's row). Haggler after freshness and without the first-sale $1; Big Basket at normal price; Charmer 2 favours a day; Tip Jar $2; Nest Egg $18.
- **Upgrades and favours:** costs 2 / 2 / 3 / 4; Juice Bar juices up to 2; 18 favour cards at 5-6 stalls.
- **Screens:** set-up and online rooms up to 6 stalls (Market League and Daily stay at 2-4); the rush pop-up seats six cards; the sales card shows hawker money and Sharp Ears tips; How to play and tutorial updated. After-sale advice follows the new tie order and allows for the night hawker.
- **Print kit:** regenerated (`print/kit/`): lorry cards with 5 rows, 8 shopkeepers, 20 events, 5 guests, 18 favours; all layout checks pass.
- **Release plumbing:** RULES_VERSION 11; new daily seeds; rules 10 saves are not resumed.

Choices I made where the spec was open: Rival Roll Call still counts the original six shopkeepers, so nobody's progress changes. The Sharp Ears card text is slightly shorter than the spec so it fits the Stall Sign; RULES.md keeps the full wording.
