## 4. What is left for the owner

Three choices, none urgent; each was tested on a copy or measured, and none is built.

1. **The Charmer starts with 3 favour cards, not 4.** This puts every shopkeeper within 4 points at 2, 3, 4 and 6 stalls with Normal computers (largest distance 2.6 / 3.1 / 1.8 / 3.7). Not yet run with Hard computers. Recommended.
2. **The Ripening Room's $1 discount on green mangoes.** The owner chose it, but it largely undoes the $4 green mango: stalls with the room pay about $3.40 to $3.55, 50 to 90% of computers build it, and it is the strongest upgrade (+$4.13 a game). Options: keep it; or drop the discount and keep the faster ripening.
3. **Mango standing orders.** About half find no bidder (41.5 / 53.4 / 46.0% at 3 / 4 / 6 stalls, against 17.6% before), because the $12 stake and the green mangoes must be paid for at once. A $9 stake helps only a little. Options: leave it (an order nobody wants is simply skipped); or look for another fix.

Noted, no decision asked: Free Fruit is the strongest favour (+$4.92 a copy). The Handcart loses a Normal computer $2. Mango Windfall still costs a stall $1.57 on the day. Tourist Bus does nothing at 5 and 6 stalls. One Hard computer among Normal ones now wins 73 / 67 / 60% at 2 / 3 / 4 stalls, because Hard remembers every purchase and Normal only today's. Big Basket is -3.7 at 6 stalls.
