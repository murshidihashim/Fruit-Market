## 1. Where we start

- **The League today** (PLAN-115) has six stages of 6 days each, built on the same engine. A stage can set:
  - which event comes on which day;
  - which standing order comes on which Market Day;
  - which customers come on which day;
  - a rival who always undercuts by $1.

  Each stage has a "win" clear and three optional stars, 18 stars in all, plus a League stars trophy card with three tiers.
- **What the engine cannot do yet:**
  - start a stage with stock already on the shelf, or with set cash;
  - start part-way through a day, for example at the bell or at the standing-order auction;
  - run 1 to 4 days;
  - switch off a rule part for a stage (favours, upgrades);
  - judge a goal other than winning, for example "$8 of watermelon sales" or "deliver in full with a margin".

## 2. Each mode's job (the owner's brief)

| Mode | Why a player taps it | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal game | "A full market, my way" | Computer, One phone, rooms and Quick match grouped under one heading in the Play menu. Nothing else changes |
| Market League | "What does the next market throw at me" | The campaign and its road (PLAN-118) |
| Daily market | "How do I rank in today's full market" | Keeps its full game and weekly twist; later shows card combinations already met in the League (PLAN-119) |
| Market Sprint | "A few minutes; I can beat that score" | Keeps three days and unlimited retries; the weekly ranking counts each player's best 5 days of the week (PLAN-119) |

## 3. PLAN-118 · The League pilot: 12 stages, the road and three mastery trophies (release 1.28.0)

### Stages

- **Built from data, on the same engine.** Each stage says:
  - its stalls and rivals (with their levels and habits);
  - your starting cash and stock;
  - the starting day and phase;
  - its length (1 to 6 days);
  - the cards, by day;
  - which rule parts are on;
  - its goals.
- **Chapter 1, Village Square:** the six current stages plus six new ones.
  - Reading a rival: Rosa buys three watermelons for a customer who wants two.
  - Selling before it spoils: start with Soft blueberries and an Okay watermelon.
  - Timing the mangoes: green mangoes, with a premium buyer due in two days.
  - Bidding on a contract: open at the auction.
  - Rescuing the stall: $5, ageing stock and a rival in the lead.
  - A four-stall finale that uses all of them.
- **Short stages open at the decision.** A pricing puzzle opens with the lorry unloaded and the rivals' purchases visible.
- **Every new stage is checked.** A balance run shows it can be cleared, is not trivial, and that its best play differs from the stage before it.

### Stars and the road

- **Stars:** 1 for the clear and up to 2 for optional goals, so 3 per stage. The six current stages are converted, and stars you have already earned are kept.
- **The League road:**
  - the next stage you can play;
  - your best stars on each stage;
  - the next trophy within reach;
  - expert stages that open with stars.
- **On results:** exactly which goal you missed, and a Retry button.

### Mastery trophies

Each trophy says something about how you play:

- **Contract Trader:** you can bid and deliver.
- **Night Market Regular:** you can turn ageing stock into cash.
- **Market Reader:** you read rivals' buying.

Each trophy:

- shows its title on your stall, in rooms and on the Daily board;
- gives an existing Wardrobe item that is already drawn, so nothing waits on new art.

### What does not change

The rules (version 20) and the stamp amounts of ordinary games stay the same. Chapters open by League clears, never by stall level.

## 4. PLAN-119 · Each mode's job (release 1.28.0)

- **Play menu:** a "Normal game" group (Computer, One phone, rooms, Quick match), then League, Daily market and Sprint, each with its one-line reason.
- **Sprint:**
  - The weekly ranking counts your best 5 days of the week, so missing two days does not hurt.
  - Results show your personal best, your place and one decisive moment from the Rush. The reason line already exists; it is renamed and made sharper.
- **Links from the League:**
  - after a pricing chapter, a "try it in today's Sprint" link;
  - Daily twists that use combinations from chapters you have finished: a standing-order day, a Pasar Malam day.

  These choose among the Daily's existing twists and cards, so the Daily's rules do not change.

## 5. Then: chapters of ten

- Each later chapter is its own small plan: the stages, a recurring rival, one market condition and a finale.
- The chapter names follow the stall ranks (Roadside Table to Towkay), two chapters per rank.
- We measure how long a chapter takes to write and balance, and which stages players replay. Then we decide whether the full 120 is worth it.
- New chapters need no change to the rules; any that would need one goes to the owner first.

## 7. Risks and costs

- **Effort:** the pilot is about the size of PLAN-115 again. The engine options are the risky part, and tests confirm they change nothing outside the League.
- **Old progress:** stars and stage numbers move to the new numbering, and nothing earned is lost.
- **Server:** the weekly Sprint ranking change keeps the same data and only counts it differently.
- **Cost:** RM0. No new service and no new art, apart from short character moments reusing the existing art.
