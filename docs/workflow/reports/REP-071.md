## WHAT CHANGED

The Wholesale Market is now the look-D board: a pastel lane per fruit, paper slots with a coin-shaped price and a freshness corner flag, a faint fruit in empty slots, and a gold outline on the slots the lorry delivers into. The lorry is a flat smooth drawing (an inline SVG) on a road strip under the queue; the old picture file is removed. Rival stalls and your stall are paper cards with striped awnings; the top bar, chips and the bar along the bottom are ink-outlined pills; the table sits on a cream mat. Trays and pop-ups keep their dark look until PLAN-033. Two scenery colours (street green, road) were added to the palette.

## ALSO FIXED

The e2e, perf and smoke scripts clicked the old button labels from before the rename ("Open the shops", "Open the market"); they now click "Start game" and "Start buying". The tutorial text said "Tap Open the market"; it now says "Tap Start buying", and the retired-name test covers those labels.
