## 1. What changed from REP-142

- **One character, no boxes.** Rosa stands on the left behind her counter, with a soft arch of her own colour behind her and her crate in front. The stack of framed rivals is gone.
- **She moves.** She rises from behind the counter, sways, blinks, waves her price tag and talks; the fruit in her crate hops.
- **She explains the game.** Her bubble shows four short lines in turn; a tap on her shows the next. She does not change when the mode changes.
- **9:16 phone.** The mockup is 360 x 640, upright. "16:9" was read as the common upright phone shape, because the game is played upright.
- The profile chip is back beside the title, as today. The mode names are a full-width row under the cards, as today's tabs.
- The button routes are as in REP-142, section 3, except that the profile chip stays and a tap on Rosa only shows her next line.

## 2. Her lines (new words, suggested)

1. Welcome! · I'm Rosa. This is my stall.
2. Buy low · Each morning the lorry brings fruit. Buy what you can sell.
3. Sell smart · Set your prices in secret. The best offer gets the customer.
4. Most cash wins · Fruit spoils, so sell it in time. Now pick a game!

## 3. A correction to REP-142

REP-142 said blinking and waving need new drawings. That was wrong. Rosa's picture
(`public/art-ticket/scenes/rosa.svg`) is made of separate shapes, and the mockup moves her eyes, head, arm,
flower and mouth using that picture alone. No new art is needed for her.

## 4. Limits

- Each shopkeeper would need the same set-up by hand. One host is the small version; using the shopkeeper you play as means eight.
- On 360 x 640 the cards are 166 px wide and carry less text than today.
- Quick Match goes from one tap to two.
- The mockup was looked at at 360 x 640 only. Wider and narrower screens: NOT RUN. Game tests: NOT APPLICABLE.
