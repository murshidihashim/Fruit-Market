## WHAT CHANGED

Uncle Toad is replaced by **Mak Jenab**. She keeps the Nest Egg perk (start with $15 instead of $10): a thrifty stallholder who saves every sen. Her portrait is a human with a purple headscarf (tudung) and a teal baju, in the same pixel style. Her computer-shop lines are warm and thrifty. The other five characters and all perks are unchanged. The frog sprite is removed.

## WHY HER

The owner left the choice of character and attributes open. The saver perk fits her, and keeping an already balanced perk avoids a rebalance.
