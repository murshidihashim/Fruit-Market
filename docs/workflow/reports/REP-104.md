## 2. How this answers the request

- **One card, app and print.** The app's row ticket is the printed customer card with the quirk and footer left off; nothing else changes place. A tap opens the whole card. Events, favours, upgrades, shopkeepers, lorries and standing orders become cards too, where the app today uses chips, list rows and pills.
- **Portraits centred.** Each picture is framed on the face; the build will keep a focus point for every picture so no face is cut at the eyes or chin.
- **Less to remember.** On screen without a tap: the buying order, today's buys, what is wanted, what tonight does to each piece (including a Heatwave), how fresh the crate is and what it cost, and the tie order.
- **Fewer steps.** The rush is one card a customer. Stocking with nothing ripe and evenings with no choice are skipped (decided in DEC-086; not drawn).
