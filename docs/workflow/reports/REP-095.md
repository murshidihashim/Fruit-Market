## 1. What "this design" is

The six-phone design drawn on 2026-11-10 for art style option C (Market Ticket): Morning, Buying, Stocking, The bell, The rush and Night falls, one phone each. Its source is kept outside the repo; a picture of it was sent to the owner as `ui.png`.

A first attempt on this branch kept the 1.16.0 screens and changed colours, the market columns and the night panel. The owner rejected it. This report covers the second build, where each in-game screen was laid out again from the design.

## 2. What was built, screen by screen

| Part | Now |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Black bar: DAY 2 / 10, today's event on a yellow tag with its picture, your rank (2ND), your cash on a white tag. |
| Beats | A fixed strip under the bar: 1 Morning, 2 Buying, 3 Stocking, 4 Bell, 5 Rush. Today's beat is yellow, finished beats black. The green stamp that covered the customers is gone (kept only for "Final 3 days" and "Final day"). |
| Customers | Tickets on a kraft band: the name on a black band, the character's portrait with the whole face showing and the card number on it, the F O S lamps down the picture's edge, and one row per fruit (how many, the most they pay). Face-down walk-ins show only the wanted fruit in boxes. |
| Rivals | Square boxes: the stall's pattern with the shopkeeper's face, name, cash and what they are doing (choosing, ready, pricing, sealed, then the price and result in the rush). |
| Morning | Today's news in a red frame: the event's picture, its name, what it changes in big figures, then the rule. Under it, what the lorry unloaded into each lane, and tomorrow's lorry. |
| Buying | The Wholesale Market as four lanes in the fruit's colour, freshest box at the top, each box with its letter, the pieces in it and the price. A box you can buy from has a black border. |
| Stocking | "Fill your crate": one row for each box of your shelf that holds fruit, with − and + and the number in the crate. Your crate stays in view at the top. |
| The bell | "Every crate is open" (each rival's crate), then "Your secret prices": one row per fruit with its own − and +, how many are out and wanted, and how many would buy at your price. |
| The rush | In the middle of the table, not over it: the customer on a band in the colour of the fruit they want, with their portrait; a box per stall whose price is sealed until the boxes turn over together; then the result and why. |

| Legend | Under the market: Green, Fresh, Okay, Soft with their letters (the owner asked for a legend in place of a sentence). |

| Pop-ups | A customer's own ticket (large, with their line), a rival's stall, today's news again, the pause menu and the tips use the same boxes. |

Nothing in this package changes a rule. (Rules version 15, secret stalls, was built on the same branch afterwards: REP-096.)

## 3. Where the app differs from the picture, and why

| Design picture | App | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Text-only customer tickets, first names | Portraits, full names on up to two lines | The owner asked for the character designs; the printed cards carry them. |
| Lanes: blueberry, watermelon, apple, mango; letter T for Tired; shelf of 6 | Blueberry, watermelon, mango, apple; S for Soft; shelf of 8; lanes of 3, 4, 5 and 6 boxes | The printed kit and rules version 14. |
| Stall patterns only | Pattern with the shopkeeper's face on it | Keeps the shopkeepers on screen; the patterns are the six printed stall sheets. |
| Night shows the market only | Market and your shelf, side by side | Your own fruit ageing is what you plan from; on the table both slide. |
| "Next customer" button in the rush | The rush still plays by itself; tap to hurry, Skip to finish | Unchanged behaviour from 1.16.0. |
| No evening or standing-order screen | Both kept, drawn in the same boxes | They exist in the game (favours, upgrades, rules 14 orders). |
| No "Tomorrow" strip | Tomorrow's customers and the standing order stay on the kraft band | Needed to plan. |

## 4. Removed

- The drawn lorry driving in and out (fruit still flies into the lanes).
- Customers and rivals swapping rows at the bell: every row now keeps its place all day.
- The wooden crates, the card frames on the table, the old shelf chips and the green beat stamp.
- The three-column layout on a wide screen: the table is now one phone-width column, centred.

## 5. Not redesigned

Home, New game, Play a friend, results, How to play, Trophies, Settings and the wardrobe keep the 1.16.0 layouts with the Market Ticket colours.

## 7. Risks

- The wide-screen layout lost its three columns.
- On a small phone the middle scrolls in the two cases above.
- Worn looks (regalia) are drawn at 22 px or larger only; on the 30 px rival boxes they are small.
- The balance findings of REP-094 are unchanged (untuned).

On 2026-11-10 the owner was told the design canvas holds these six screens as separate pages. It does not: the canvas pages were later replaced by the second version (the one without Night falls). The picture `ui.png` is the record of this design.
