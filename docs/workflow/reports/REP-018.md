## The idea in one line
**Pixel heart, smooth frame.** The first toy-box mockups (the cosy ones) are back, but the characters,
fruit and icons are now the game's own pixel art, and headings use the game's pixel font.

## What changed from the cunning edition
- **Cosy restored:** the first toy-box layout and warm colours (cream, teal, wood, terracotta).
  Your Evening layout edit is kept. The dark plum screens, curtain and spotlight are gone.
- **Pixel art:** the fruit at four freshness stages, Grandpa Tan, Cowboy John, Foxy Rosa, Patches,
  you, the upgrades, bell, coin and favour card all use the game's own sprites (built from
  `src/ui/pixel.tsx`). Foxy Rosa and Patches get sly half-closed eyes.
- **Light sly touch, in warm colours:** dotted "Psst…" whispers from Foxy Rosa (buying, pricing),
  "Seal your prices" and "only you see this", "Outfoxed by $1!" on the reveal, "…and every price is a
  secret" on the home sign, with Foxy Rosa peeking out from behind it.

## Why this is cheaper to build
The full vector redraw (DEC-021) is no longer needed. The build reuses and tidies the existing sprites;
most of the work is the new frame and layout.

## Limits
- Mockups, not the game; numbers are examples. No sound yet.
- The sly eyes for Foxy Rosa and Patches are mockup variants; the build would add them as sprite variants.
