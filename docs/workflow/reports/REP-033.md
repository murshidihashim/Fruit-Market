## 1. What was built

**The day (five beats).**
1. **Dawn:** the lorry unloads; the Tomorrow strip shows the next lorry, event, expected customer and the face-down
   walk-ins (fruit icons on the backs).
2. **Market:** buying, poorest shop first.
3. **Open stalls (new):** each shop secretly fills its for-sale crate; all crates show together.
4. **The bell:** one secret price per fruit, revealed together, with a short pause before the rush.
5. **The rush:** customers buy from the cheapest stall, leftover demand spills to the next; then evening and night.

**Prototype A (core market).**
- Uniform freshness: −\$1 okay, −\$2 not so, for every fruit.
- Ties: Favoured Trade, then Haggler, then fresher fruit, then poorer shop, then turn order.
- Cash catch-up kept ("poorest buys first").
- Options in the set-up: **Core market** (no traits, favours or specialities), **Street prices** (beginner) and
  **Market pulse** (advanced).

**Prototype B (four specialities),** drafted on the evenings after days 3, 6, 9 and 12: Cold Room \$5, Juice Bar \$4,
Barrow \$6, Premium Display \$8. Shop Bell, Signboard, Bigger Shelf and Handcart are gone.

**Prototype C (rhythm).** Market Days on days 3, 6, 9 and 12 (+1 walk-in, no lorry); Last Call on the final day (+\$1,
then the jam maker buys leftovers at \$1). Set-up toggle "Market Days", on by default.

**Market Gossip** replaces Free Cash (2 copies, evening): secretly see tomorrow's walk-ins. The fronts appear only on
your screen, with a 👂 badge.

**PLAN-014 items.**
- Printed farmer cards (2–4 per fruit for 3 shops; 2 shops −1, 4 shops +1; missing fruit 1).
- Walk-ins: players − 2, then players − 1 in the second half. Customer cards are no longer scaled.
- Price shifts: blueberry −1, apple −2, mango +2.
- Events: Blueberry Glut and Apple Blight replace Slow Season and Rainy Day; Good Harvest +1 per 2 shops; Bad Harvest
  rounds up; no Health Inspector on the final day.
- `RULES_VERSION` 4. Older apps are asked to update; daily scores are kept separately per rules version.

**App feedback.**
- Stall screen and price screen: each shows rivals' crates, which customers can pay your price, and a hint.
- Day ledger: sales, bought, other (fines, favours), net, and fruit spoiling tonight; jam maker money on the results.
- Missed-sale reason "too old for \$X". Bell pause before the rush. New tutorial tips.
- `RULES.md` and the in-app How to play rewritten.

**One rule detail added for privacy.** The evening is now held whenever anyone has a favour card. Before, it was held
only when someone had an *evening* card, which told rivals what that player held. No balance effect.

**Specialities (3 players, fair 33%).**
- Rushing **Barrow** wins 29% and **Premium Display** 27%; skipping either costs a lot (23%, 26%).
- Rushing **Cold Room** wins only 13%, **Juice Bar** 17%. The computers buy Juice Bar rarely (0.14 per game).
- Never building anything wins 19%, so specialities are worth having.
- This matches REP-032's warning: Cold Room and Juice Bar change *what you do*, and the computers don't play those
  plans well yet, so these numbers undersell them. [Hyp] Check in the playtest whether people find them useful.

**Market feel.**
- 90% of computer sales are within \$1 of the customer's top price, and deep price wars are rare (0.1% of sales).
  Spoilage is near zero.
- Supply is tight enough that the computers rarely need to undercut. [Hyp] People may still undercut; watch for it
  in the playtest. If not, the next lever is +1 supply per listed fruit (needs a new plan).
- Market Days are lean days (income dips on days 3, 6 and 9), as designed.

## 5. Risks and open items

1. **Cold Room and Juice Bar are weak for computers.** Possible retune: Cold Room \$4, Juice Bar \$3. Not changed
   without your approval.
2. **Little undercutting among computers.** The hook ("how low dare I go?") needs a human check.
3. **2-player blowouts at 34%.** Watch in playtests.
4. **Hard vs Normal 56%,** below the old 65% aim. The computers are fair practice, not experts.
5. **Online settings:** online rooms use the defaults (Market Days on, no Core, Street or Pulse options). The playtest
   toggles are for games on one phone.
