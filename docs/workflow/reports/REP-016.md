## Limits of the mockups
- They're **mockups, not the game**: the numbers are examples, and only the parts needed to show the
  idea are interactive.
- Characters other than Grandpa Tan, Patches, Foxy Rosa and Cowboy John are not drawn yet; the build would
  redraw all of them in this style.
- I didn't add sound; that comes with the build.
- The style is original. *It Takes Two* informed the qualities only.
