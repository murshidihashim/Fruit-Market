Fruit Market: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Fruit Market is a game made by murshidihashim. Accounts are optional. It has no ads and no third-party trackers: what it sends goes only to the game's own server.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved game, statistics, achievements, stamp book, looks and daily challenge scores are kept in the app's local storage. The statistics are plain counts: games finished and won, wins with each shopkeeper, pieces sold of each fruit, standing orders delivered in full and your personal bests in one game. Your stall level is worked out from these counts. The app also notes your last 12 finished games on the device, so that no game is counted twice; these notes are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this. If you have an account, a copy of your progress is also kept on our server.

Accounts (optional)

You can play without an account. If you make one, you choose an ID and a password. We do not ask for an email address.

We store your ID, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your progress: the counts described above, achievements, unlocked looks, the looks you chose (stall colours, sign frame, title, price tag, results stamp and sounds) and league stage. This lets your progress follow you to another device. It is kept on our game server (Cloudflare). We cannot read your password and we cannot recover it if you lose it, so please keep it safe.

Please do not use your real name or email address as your ID. Offensive IDs are blocked.

To sign out, use Settings. Signing out saves your progress to your account and clears it from that device. To delete your account, use Settings and choose Delete account, then enter your password. This removes your ID, password and saved progress from our server.

Your ID is not shown to other players. Other players online see your player name and the look you are wearing: your colours and items, sign frame, title, price tag and results stamp. The sounds you chose are heard only by you and are not sent.

Game data (optional)

If "Share game data" is on in Settings (it is on by default), the app sends our server two things.

Counts. Short event counts such as "a game started", "day 5 was reached", "a game was won", "the first piece was bought", "a mode's page was opened" or "this device is active 3 days after installing" (the install date itself stays on your device), plus short error messages if the app crashes. Online, it also counts that a public room was opened or joined from the list, that a room was started with computers, and that a reaction was sent (never which reaction or by whom). These are added to daily totals, which are deleted after 90 days.

Match records. When a game ends or is left, a record of it: its set-up (mode, number of stalls, length, the computer players' levels and each stall's perk), what each stall bought, put out and charged each day, how each sale went, each stall's cash, how long each of your moves took, how often you took a move back, which rule page or card you opened, the step you left at, and a coarse note of the device (phone, tablet or desktop; Android, iOS, Windows, Mac or Linux; a rounded screen size; the app version). An online match is recorded by the game server only if every person in it has "Share game data" on. We use these records to balance the game. They are kept for about 12 months and then deleted.

Counts and match records contain no name, account, room code, device ID, advertising ID or location, and we do not store IP addresses with them. The name you gave your stall is not in a record. Turn the setting off to stop sending both.

A player number (only if you say yes)

The game asks once whether it may give your phone a random player number. If you say No, nothing in this section happens. If you say Yes, a random number is made on your device and sent with your match records, together with a random number for each time you open the app, the day you first played, your stall level, your league stage and how many looks you have unlocked. This lets us see that several games were played by the same player, which shows how players improve and what keeps the game fun. The number also puts you in one of two test groups, so that a later change can be compared fairly; today both groups play the same game.

The player number is not your name, your account ID or any ID of your phone, and it is never shown to other players. It does make it possible to tell that two records came from the same player, which the rest of this page's data cannot do.

You can turn it off in Settings ("Link my games") at any time. Forget my data in Settings deletes your player number and every match record and app-open line kept under it from our server, and removes the number from your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data also removes the number from your device.

Online play

When you play online, the name you type, the look you are wearing and your moves are sent to our game server (Cloudflare) so the other players can see them. A random seat code is stored on your device so you can reconnect. Rooms are deleted 24 hours after the last move.

If you make a public room, your player name, the number of seats taken, the game length and how long the room has waited are shown in the Public rooms list to anyone looking for a game, until the game starts or the room fills or closes. A private room is never listed.

Reactions in an online room are chosen from a fixed list of emoji and short lines; there is no free text. A reaction is passed to the other players in the room and is not stored. Who you mute is kept on your device only.

Daily challenge leaderboard

When you finish a daily challenge while online, the name you chose, your score and your moves for that game are sent to our server so it can check the score and show it on that day's leaderboard. To stop others using your name on the board, your device also sends a random key made on your device; the server stores only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, which cannot identify you. Leaderboards show names and scores only. Offensive names are replaced automatically.

Permissions

The app uses the internet for online play, the leaderboard and (optionally) game data, and may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not ask for a name, an email address, an age or a location. The optional player number above is a random number, used only as described there. If you are a parent and want it removed, use Forget my data in Settings or write to us. Players should not use their real full name or email address as their player name or ID.
