Fruit Market: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fruit Market is a single-device game made by Murshidi bin Md Hashim.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, saved game, statistics and daily challenge scores are kept only in the app's local storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

The app may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
